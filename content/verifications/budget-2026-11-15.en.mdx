## Verification carried out on 15 November 2026

**Result: two key figures clarified, no wrong figure.**

### Method

The sources of the card's 25 key figures, of its summary and of its two status boxes were opened one by one, and each number was compared with the text of its source. Twenty-two pages and documents were read, including the Court of Audit's 31st Book and its press release.

### Corrections applied

- 2025 deficit: the card showed "1.6 billion" without saying that this is the figure given by the government on 4 October 2026, alongside the realised ESA financing balance of -1,027.9 million calculated by the National Accounts Institute and taken up by the Court of Audit. The description now distinguishes the two measures. The source cited for this figure is also corrected: the VRT NWS article does not give it, those of RTBF and BRUZZ do.
- Personal income tax cut: the card kept only the February 2026 estimate, about 268 euros a year. In July 2026, the Budget minister's office gave examples of 147 and 152 euros a year. Both estimates now appear side by side.

### Confirmed

Targeted deficit of EUR 719 million in 2027 and EUR 957 million in 2026, 2026 revenue and expenditure, vote on the 2026 budget (53 yes, 32 no), UNamur projection for 2029, consolidated gross debt of EUR 17.7 billion, 2025 ESA balance, interest charges, S&P rating, credit lines, objectives of the government agreement, COCOM budget and deficit, administrative reform, cost of the tax cut, municipal surcharges on the property tax. The Court of Audit had also issued an adverse opinion on the 2024 accounts: the 2025 one is indeed the second in a row. The plenary vote on the 2027 budget on 18 December is reported by BRUZZ as likely.

### Limits

Two figures could not be reread and are kept as they are: the deficit of EUR 416 million planned for 2028 (Le Soir article reserved for subscribers) and the 791,621 tax returns (not found on the IBSA page cited, which refers to tables). The 17.2% rise in the City of Brussels surcharge is a calculation from the two published rates. The sections of the body earlier than 1 October 2026 and the BX1 sources were not reread.
