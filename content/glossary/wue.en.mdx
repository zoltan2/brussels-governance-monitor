WUE measures how much water a data centre withdraws for cooling, set against the energy consumed by its IT equipment. A WUE of 1.0 litre per kilowatt-hour means one litre of water is withdrawn for every kilowatt-hour of computing.

The same site can show a good PUE and a poor WUE. Evaporative water cooling is energy-efficient but water-hungry; air cooling uses more electricity and almost no water. The two indicators must therefore be read together.

The revised draft of the EU regulation, published on **2 July 2026**, widened the measurement: WUE covered **potable water**, and now covers **freshwater**, bringing withdrawals of non-potable water into the calculation, such as the canal water used by some Belgian sites.
