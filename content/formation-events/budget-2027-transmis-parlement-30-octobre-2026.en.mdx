## The fact

The Brussels government has sent the 2027 budget documents to Parliament, after their approval at an electronic council of ministers, the budget minister confirmed on **Friday 30 October 2026**. The targeted deficit is **719 million euros**, for a budgetary effort of **563 million**.

## What is announced next

Committee scrutiny follows the autumn recess; the plenary vote is envisaged for Friday 18 December 2026. Detail in the [Budget](/en/domains/budget) card.
