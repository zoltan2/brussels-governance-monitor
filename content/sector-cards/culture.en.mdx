## Kanal: fire safety tests passed, opening maintained on 28 November (8 October 2026)

The Brussels fire service carried out a safety inspection on the Kanal construction site. It took place on Wednesday 7 October 2026 according to its spokesperson, quoted by BRUZZ. All the tests were assessed positively and a favourable opinion will be delivered to the City of Brussels. The decision on the opening rests with the mayor. The museum opens to the public on **28 November 2026**.

Sources: [BRUZZ (8 October 2026)](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/cultuurnieuws/brandweer-brussel-geeft-positief-veiligheidsadvies-voor-opening-kanal-2026-10-08).

## Coalition Agreement: Announced Commitments
According to concordant press sources, the agreement of 12 February 2026 announces measures for the cultural sector:

- **Kanal**: **EUR 60 million** according to La Libre, **EUR 50 million** according to La DH, in regional contribution for the opening of the contemporary art museum (former Citroen garage, 40,000 m²). Opening scheduled for **28 November 2026** after 9 years of construction. Total project cost: **EUR 230 million** (regional investment: EUR 185M, 95% disbursed). Annual operating budget: **EUR 30 million** (degressive, with a requirement to seek own revenue). Centre Pompidou partnership: EUR 12.5M until 2031. First exhibition: "A truly immense journey" (350+ works, Nov 2026 → Jan 2028)
- **Review of discretionary subsidies**: all regional subsidy envelopes will be re-examined as part of the fiscal consolidation effort of around EUR 1 billion

**Point of attention**: the support for Kanal (EUR 50 to 60 million depending on the source) is a major investment, but the systematic review of discretionary subsidies could affect other regional cultural institutions. The details of the overall cultural arbitration are not yet known.

## Fire at the Kanal construction site (6 July 2026)

On **6 July 2026** at around 6pm, a fire broke out in a technical room under the roof of the Kanal construction site, affecting around **400 sqm** of the "Kanal Architecture" building (intended for archives and collections). Firefighters brought the blaze under control by around 9pm, with no injuries. The cause is presumed accidental; a police and prosecutor's investigation is under way, and a structural assessment of the roof is planned.

The Fondation Kanal's director general, Yves Goldstein, confirmed that the museum's **general opening remains scheduled for 28 November 2026**, but that the "Kanal Architecture" building will not be ready for the inauguration.

Sources: [BX1](https://bx1.be/categories/news/un-incendie-sest-declare-sur-le-chantier-du-musee-kanal/) (6 July 2026); [La Libre](https://www.lalibre.be/regions/bruxelles/2026/07/06/incendie-au-musee-kanal-intervention-des-pompiers-en-cours-ZOLTPCQPFJAKTJSD5LHORCG6UM/) (6 July 2026); [The Art Newspaper](https://www.artnewspaper.fr/2026/07/08/le-futur-musee-kanal-pompidou-a-bruxelles-endommage-par-un-incendie) (8 July 2026).

## Standoff at the top of government over Kanal's leadership (17 September 2026)

At the Brussels cabinet meeting of **17 September 2026**, the Minister-President demanded the immediate dismissal of the Fondation Kanal's director general, **Yves Goldstein**, less than three months before the museum's opening, scheduled for **28 November 2026**. The Deputy Minister-President opposed the move, citing legal and operational risks, and blocked the decision.

According to press reports, the director general was reportedly offered an ultimatum: an immediate departure, or staying on with reduced powers until the museum's opening.

The budgetary context reported by the press points to an annual allocation of **28 million euros** in 2026, set to fall to **10 million euros** by 2029.

Sources: [BRUZZ](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/politiek/minister-president-dillies-wil-kanal-baas-goldstein-ontslaan-2026-09-18) (18 September 2026); [La Libre](https://www.lalibre.be/belgique/politique-belge/2026/09/17/bras-de-fer-au-sommet-du-gouvernement-bruxellois-boris-dillies-veut-limoger-le-patron-de-kanal-mais-se-heurte-a-ahmed-laaouej-DZ6CID55V5EOTORHZRUIW7FSWY/) (17 September 2026). Confidence: estimated. The meeting was held behind closed doors, no official statement was published, and both accounts rest on anonymous government sources.

## Kanal: director general's departure set for 30 November 2026 (21 September 2026)

On **Monday 21 September 2026**, the Minister-President's office announced an agreement with the director general of **Kanal-Centre Pompidou**, Yves Goldstein: his assignment will end on **30 November 2026**, two days after the museum's inauguration, which remains set for **Saturday 28 November 2026**. According to the statement, the parties note "a divergence of views on the future of KANAL" and have agreed "to clarify the terms and timetable for the end of his assignment, which he had already announced". The director general remains in office until that date, but will not take part in the selection process for his successor.

According to The Art Newspaper, he had announced in early May 2026 that he would leave at the end of his second five-year term, staying in post until a successor was appointed, expected in early 2027. RTBF reports that the museum's leadership had caused sharp tensions within the regional majority in the days before the announcement. The statement does not mention the ultimatum reported by the press after the cabinet meeting of 17 September, which remains unconfirmed.

A Kanal spokesperson confirmed to The Art Newspaper the regional allocation of **28 million euros** in 2026 and its reduction to **10 million euros** a year by 2029, adding that the latter amount will only apply after discussion within the government. According to the same newspaper, Kanal pays the Centre Pompidou **2 million euros a year** until 2031 under their partnership. Also according to the museum, the **30,000 free tickets** offered on reservation for the opening weekend were all taken on the first day they went on sale.

Sources: [RTBF](https://www.rtbf.be/article/yves-goldstein-quittera-la-direction-generale-du-musee-kanal-le-30-novembre-prochain-11788228) (21 September 2026); [BX1](https://bx1.be/categories/news/kanal-yves-goldstein-restera-en-fonction-jusqua-louverture-du-musee/) (21 September 2026); [The Art Newspaper](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2026/09/23/yves-goldstein-to-depart-as-director-general-of-brussels-upcomin%67-kanal-museum-immediately-following-opening-weekend) (23 September 2026). Confidence: official for the agreement, the departure date and the unchanged inauguration date (statement from the Minister-President's office, carried by Belga). Estimated for the May announcement, the amounts and the ticketing, reported by The Art Newspaper alone.

## Kanal: five of twelve safety tests inconclusive according to Apache, SIAMU inspection on 8 October (28 September 2026)

According to Apache, quoted by BRUZZ on **28 September 2026**, **five of the twelve safety tests** carried out at Kanal the previous week (including fire safety, access control and electrics) were not conclusive. The Brussels fire service (**SIAMU**) confirms an inspection on **Thursday 8 October 2026** and states that its opinion is advisory, the decision on the opening resting with the mayor. Kanal says the opening on **28 November 2026** is not at risk.

Source: [BRUZZ](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/cultuurnieuws/kanal-slaagde-nog-niet-voor-brandveiligheid-op-8-oktober-nieuwe-test-2026-09-28) (28 September 2026). Confidence: unconfirmed. The test results come from Apache and are reported by BRUZZ alone.

## Flanders cuts its cultural subsidies to the Brussels municipalities by 30% (2 October 2026)

From **2027**, the Flemish government is cutting by **30%** the operating subsidies for the Dutch-language cultural policy of the Brussels municipalities and for their Dutch-language libraries. The spokesperson for the Flemish Minister of Culture confirmed this to BRUZZ on **Friday 2 October 2026**: the funds provided for the Brussels municipalities under the Flemish decree on supralocal culture (Bovenlokaal Cultuurdecreet) fall from **€3.7 million to €2.6 million**. The Brussels aldermen concerned were informed by email. The Flemish minister responsible for Brussels says she is in discussions to see whether and how this saving can be offset.

This decision comes on top of the withdrawal of Flemish subsidies from Bozar (see the section of 1 October 2026). The Flemish Community Commission (VGC), for its part, puts the effect of the Flemish savings on its own funds in 2027 at about one million euros (see the [Institutional](/en/domains/institutional) card).

In the same series of savings, the economic fund **Screen Flanders** is abolished from **1 January 2027**: its budget of **€3.5 million** is withdrawn in full, the Vlaams Audiovisueel Fonds (VAF) confirms to BRUZZ. Created in 2012, the fund supports audiovisual productions on condition that the amount granted is spent in the Flemish Region; all Belgian producers are eligible. Brussels films and series have drawn on it, according to BRUZZ. The VAF says it hopes the decision will be reviewed.

Sources: [BRUZZ, on the municipal subsidies](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/politiek/vlaanderen-bespaart-dertig-procent-op-gemeentelijk-cultuurbeleid-en-bibliotheken-2026-10-02) (2 October 2026); [BRUZZ, on Screen Flanders](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/economie/screen-flanders-opgedoekt-ook-brusselse-films-en-series-verliezen-financieringsbron-2026-10-02) (2 October 2026). Confidence: *estimated* (single press source; figures confirmed to BRUZZ by the minister's spokesperson and by the VAF).

## Bozar loses its Flemish subsidies from 2027 (1 October 2026)

The **Centre for Fine Arts (Bozar)**, a federal cultural institution based in Brussels, will no longer receive subsidies from the Flemish Community from **2027**. The news, revealed by VRT on **Thursday 1 October 2026**, was confirmed to BRUZZ; it follows the reduction in subsidies announced by the Flemish government on Wednesday 30 September 2026 with its 2027 budget. The amount is **€760,000**, or just under 2% of the operating budget, according to BRUZZ. According to Bozar, these subsidies accounted for **1.84%** of its 2025 revenue, which stood at **€38 million**, 52% of which came from the federal allocation and the National Lottery.

Bozar's director general says he was not consulted. According to him, the federal allocation does not cover staff and operating costs, and it is the other subsidies that fund the programming. He also states that the subsidy from the Brussels-Capital Region has fallen and stands at **€150,000 a year**. The Flemish Minister of Culture calls the withdrawal "very regrettable".

In the same round of savings, the **AMVB** (Archief en Museum voor het Vlaams Leven te Brussel) loses half of its Flemish subsidy, around **€250,000** less according to its director; it also receives €247,000 from the VGC (Flemish Community Commission).

Sources: [BRUZZ](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/cultuurnieuws/bozar-verliest-alle-vlaamse-subsidies-2026-10-01) (1 October 2026); [La Libre](https://www.lalibre.be/belgique/2026/10/01/la-flandre-supprime-le-subside-quelle-accordait-a-bozar-GK472MV24NBIRD3TVAZJKHQMQI/) (1 October 2026); [BRUZZ, on the AMVB](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/cultuurnieuws/archief-en-museum-voor-vlaams-leven-te-brussel-verliest-helft-vlaamse-subsidies-bitter-2026-10-01) (1 October 2026); [Bozar, "Financial Model"](https://www.bozar.be/en/financial-model), accessed 2 October 2026. Confidence: unconfirmed. The information rests on two newsrooms (BRUZZ and La Libre); neither the Flemish decision nor Bozar's accounts were consulted, and the amount of the regional subsidy is a statement by the director general.

## Converging budgetary pressures (February 2026)

The Brussels cultural sector faces simultaneous cuts at **three levels of government**:

### Federation Wallonia-Brussels

The FWB 2026 budget, adopted in late February, provides for **EUR 255 million in total savings**, including **EUR 12.9 million** in cuts to the cultural sector (approximately 4% of a cultural budget of EUR 320M+). Concrete measures:

- **Non-indexation** of cultural subsidies in 2026 (estimated saving: **EUR 3.8 million**)
- **Moratorium on new recognitions** and accreditations until 2028 (cultural centres, public libraries, visual arts, continuing education). Estimated non-spending: **~EUR 10M** in 2026, **~EUR 15M** in 2029
- Cumulative FWB savings reach **EUR 500 million over 4 years** (2026-2029)

Source: RTBF, "le budget 2026 de la FWB definitivement adopte", February 2026; FEAS, "commissions culture et budget", February 2026.

### City of Brussels

The City of Brussels reduced its cultural budget by **19.6%** between 2024 and 2026. Concrete consequences:

- **La Centrale** (contemporary art centre, 1,000 m²): **permanently closed on 22 February 2026** after 20 years of operation. 6 of 20 employees made redundant. The building is expected to "retain a cultural use" (undefined)
- **FAME Festival** (multidisciplinary feminist festival, founded 2021): funding **completely withdrawn** in February 2026, after 4 months of work on the 2026 edition
- Reduction in subsidies to the Theatre Royal du Parc and the KVS

Source: RTBF, "La Centrale ferme ses portes", 22 Feb 2026; L'Avenir, "le festival FAME disparait", 19 Feb 2026.

### Alarm from the sector

**RAB/BKO** (Brussels Arts Network, 168 cultural actors) launched a public appeal for **financial guarantees** for 2026, denouncing an "unbearable uncertainty". Organisations have laid off staff, imposed part-time work or ceased their activities.

The **Zinneke Parade 2026** (14th edition, **30 May 2026**, theme "Dream") remains in financial uncertainty. The regional subsidy was reduced from EUR 391,000 to **EUR 360,000** (35% of total budget). The number of Zinnodes has dropped from 20 to **13** due to insufficient funding. The Brussels Parliament adopted a resolution in December 2025 calling for financial guarantees, but as of 2 March 2026, **no confirmation of structural funding** has been provided by the new government.

Source: L'Avenir, "Zinneke menacee", Oct 2025; BX1, "le Parlement veut lever l'incertitude", Dec 2025.

## BD Comic Strip Festival 2026 cancelled — Visit.Brussels in crisis (March 2026)

The 2026 edition of the **BD Comic Strip Festival** (scheduled for 25-27 September at Gare Maritime / Tour & Taxis) has been **cancelled**. The direct cause: a **EUR 5.7 million** reduction in Visit.Brussels' 2026 budget (from EUR 22M to EUR 16.3M). CEO Patrick Bontinck confirmed the cancellation, adding that the **Iris Festival** will be reduced to a single concert on 9 May and that promotion of other events is frozen (except Brussels Pride).

The announced austerity trajectory is severe: regional subsidies to Visit.Brussels would decrease from **EUR 22M to EUR 8M by 2029**, threatening **90 out of 160 jobs**. This cancellation adds to cumulative cuts across three levels of government (FWB −12.9M, City of Brussels −19.6%, Region via Visit.Brussels).

Source: BX1 / Le Soir / BRUZZ (29-31 March 2026). Confidence: official (Visit.Brussels CEO).

## Dutch-speaking cultural policy (VGC)

Alongside the French-speaking policies (FWB, COCOF), the **VGC** (Vlaamse Gemeenschapscommissie / Flemish Community Commission) manages a structured Dutch-speaking cultural network in Brussels:

- **22 community centres** (gemeenschapscentra, N22 network) — local venues for Dutch-speaking culture, youth and coexistence, spread across the 19 municipalities. The VGC is the governing authority ("inrichtende overheid")
- **19 local libraries** — one per municipality, supported by the VGC in content, management, funding and digital services
- **Muntpunt** — central Dutch-speaking cultural institution, located near the Grand-Place
- **18 municipal services** for Dutch-speaking culture

The VGC offers **arts subsidies** to organisations and individual artists in Brussels: operating subsidies (annual or three-year), project subsidies, trajectory subsidies and debut subsidies (maximum EUR 3,000 for first-time artists). Cultural policy is led by college member Ans Persoons (Vooruit).

The **Paspartoe** programme (leisure pass) facilitates access to culture for disadvantaged groups, in collaboration with community centres and libraries.

Source: [VGC, "Lokaal cultuurbeleid"](https://www.vgc.be/), accessed 1 March 2026.
