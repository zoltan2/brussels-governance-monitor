## Urban Shield extended and security measures reinforced

The federal Council of Ministers approved on **18 September** the six-month extension of the Urban Shield operation in Brussels train stations and metro stations. Launched in April 2026, this operation deploys approximately **fifty soldiers** compared to around thirty at launch. According to the cabinet of the federal Interior Minister, **884 incidents** were recorded in the metro and stations between April and July **2026**, a decline of **16.8 %** compared to the previous year (**1,062 incidents**). The Brussels Region has no control over this federal decision but bears its direct effects in public space.

Additionally, a nineteenth anti-drug zone has been created in the Gray district in Ixelles. The [City of Brussels extended the ban on alcohol consumption in the hypercentre until **6 October 2030**](/en/domains/security).

## Regional budget: structurally high debt burden

The Federal Planning Bureau considers the risk of Brussels' debt becoming unsustainable in the medium term to be **high**. Brussels' debt ratio would reach **328 %** of regional revenues in **2031**, compared to **327 %** in Wallonia. Flanders shows a moderate risk at **118 %**, the only acceptable region. This analysis confirms that Brussels and Wallonia face comparable budgetary challenges. The Wallonia-Brussels Federation, also classified as high-risk, is projected at **158 %** of its revenues in **2031**. [See the budget domain →](/en/domains/budget)

## Mobility: tensions over car-free Sunday and municipal subsidies

Woluwe-Saint-Lambert is the only one of the nineteen Brussels municipalities refusing to participate in the second car-free Sunday, scheduled for **2 May 2027**. The municipal college cites costs of **35,000 to 50,000 euros** and the mobilization of **70 to 80 police officers**. It also questions a reduction in regional cycling subsidies. The regional Mobility Minister's cabinet disputes this claim: **324,000 euros** paid in **2026** compared to **345,000** in **2025**, plus **780,000 euros** for infrastructure. Both versions are reported without consensus. Moreover, on **18 September**, taxi drivers demonstrated on boulevard Pachéco against "ten years of asphyxiation," highlighting impacts from the Good Move plan and traffic restrictions.

## Critical dossiers: PFAS, Kanal, Metro 3

**PFAS.** The ECHA's risk assessment committee classified TFA as toxic for reproduction on **5 June 2026**, a classification still requiring European Commission ratification. On **22 July 2026**, EFSA lowered the acceptable daily dose from **0.05 to 0.014 milligram per kilogram**, a reduction by roughly three and a half times. ClientEarth filed on **8 July 2026** a collective complaint against Belgium before the European Committee of Social Rights, citing the Sicli site in Uccle; admissibility will be examined in **2027**. Two announced milestones remain unmet: a detailed study and a third information session on the Sicli site.

**Kanal.** A power struggle continues within government: on **17 September**, the Prime Minister demanded the immediate dismissal of the Director-General of Kanal-Centre Pompidou, less than three months before the planned opening on **28 November 2026**. The Vice Prime Minister opposed this on legal and operational grounds. The reported annual allocation is **28 million euros in 2026**, expected to decline to **10 million** by **2029**. ## Ordnance on excessive rents: judicial victory

The Constitutional Court rejected on **17 September 2026** the appeal against the Brussels ordnance on excessive rents. In force since **May 2025**, this ordnance prohibits raising rents more than **20 %** above the reference rent and opens the Parity Local Rental Commission to tenants. A revision of the indicative scale is planned in two stages. The number of applicants varies by source, and none publish the constitutional court ruling number.

## Climate and environment: Schaerbeek calls for fossil fuel advertising ban

Schaerbeek is the third Brussels municipality requesting that the Region ban advertising for the fossil fuel industry in public space, after Saint-Gilles and Jette. The motion was adopted by the municipal council on **16 September 2026**. The text invites the regional government to study the legal and political possibilities for a regional ban.

## Josaphat brownfield: subdivision permit application under review

The subdivision permit application for the Josaphat brownfield, carried by the Eiffage-AXA consortium for the Urban Development Company (SAU), covers **6.76 hectares**: maximum **537 dwellings**, **8,560 square meters** of activities, **391 parking spaces**, and **46 %** green space. A collective demanded an unfavorable opinion at Schaerbeek's municipal council on **16 September 2026**, against the college's abstention in **2025**. The collective claims over **7,000 participations** in the public inquiry.
