## Security: a police officer shot dead in Jette

During the night of 12 to 13 October 2026, a **41-year-old** police officer from the Anti-Aggression Brigade of the Brussels-Capital/Ixelles zone was shot dead in Jette, during an intervention on a stolen car. According to the Brussels public prosecutor's office, several suspects have been arrested and an investigating judge has been assigned the case for murder. On 12 October, a man was also shot dead in rue Wiertz, in Ixelles; no link between the two incidents has been announced.

Near the Gare du Midi, clashes on 12 October 2026 led to around ten judicial arrests, including **nine minors**. Recruitment of the regional drugs commissioner is still under way, according to Belga.

## Urban planning: the Josaphat site and the freeze on undeveloped sites

On 15 October 2026, the consultation committee issued an unfavourable opinion on the subdivision permit for the Josaphat site: Schaerbeek voted against, three members abstained. The opinion is advisory; the Region decides.

Freeze on undeveloped sites: the court of appeal gives the parties until **2 November 2026** to substantiate their legal arguments. The building ban runs until 31 December 2026.

## Hospitals: the five municipalities approve the merger

The City of Brussels and Etterbeek on 12 October 2026, then Anderlecht, Ixelles and Saint-Gilles on 15 October approved the merger of five public hospitals under the name Hôpitaux Universitaires de Bruxelles, scheduled for 1 January 2027. ## Budget: road tax and online gambling

The Finance Minister is aiming for **1 July 2027** for the revised road tax. The road vignette would go on sale from 2 April 2027, at **90 to 125 euros** a year, without compensation. According to BRUZZ, the tax on online gambling remains at **11%** in Brussels, while Flanders and Wallonia are moving to 15%.

A study by CERPE (UNamur), based on the initial 2026 budget, projects a deficit of **512 million euros** in 2029, the year in which the government is aiming for balance.
