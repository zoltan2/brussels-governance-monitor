## What the indicator measures

Eurostat publishes the modal split of inland passenger transport every year (dataset tran_hv_psmod). The indicator used here is the share of **trains** and of **motor coaches, buses and trolley buses** in total passenger-kilometres travelled by passenger car, bus or coach and train.

It covers neither tram, nor metro, nor walking, nor cycling. Depending on the country, urban buses may or may not be included. The data are national: they describe Belgium as a whole, not Brussels.

The values below refer to **2024**, the latest year available in the Eurostat update of 26 August 2026.

## The data compared

| Country | Train | Coaches, buses, trolley buses | Train and bus | Car |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Austria | 13.7% | 10.1% | 23.8% | 76.2% |
| Belgium | 9.7% | 10.8% | 20.5% | 79.5% |
| France | 11.6% | 5.9% | 17.4% | 82.6% |
| Denmark | 7.7% | 9.2% | 16.9% | 83.1% |
| Germany | 10.6% | 5.3% | 15.9% | 84.1% |
| Netherlands | 11.0% | 3.0% | 14.0% | 86.0% |

Austria has the highest share in the panel, 23.8%. Belgium follows with **20.5%**, of which 9.7% by train and 10.8% by bus and coach. In the Netherlands, the share of rail (11.0%) is higher than in Belgium, but buses and coaches account for only 3.0%.

In Belgium, this share was 19.3% in 2019, 15.5% in 2020 and 15.1% in 2021, then 20.5% in 2024.

The 2024 values for Belgium, Austria, Germany and the Netherlands are Eurostat estimates.

## What about Brussels?

The European indicator says nothing about Brussels in particular. For the Region, the available measure is different: according to IBSA's Mini-Bru 2026, **27.0%** of trips made by Brussels residents in 2024 were by metro, tram or bus, and 2.0% by train. This is a share of the number of trips, not of kilometres travelled, taken from the BELDAM surveys and Brussels Mobility: the two figures are not comparable. The detail is in the [Mobility page](/en/domains/mobility).

STIB recorded **396.1 million journeys** in 2025, according to its press release of 27 May 2026.

## Stakes for Brussels

The regional policy declaration of 13 February 2026 provides for running trams, "instead of the metro initially planned", in the tunnels of the Albert-Nord axis near the Gare du Midi. The detail is in the [Metro 3 dossier](/en/dossiers/metro-3).
