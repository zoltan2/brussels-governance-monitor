## Kanal, the regional museum of modern and contemporary art

Kanal, devoted to modern and contemporary art, architecture and landscape, occupies the former Citroën garage on Place de l'Yser. It is run by the Kanal Foundation, in partnership with the Centre Pompidou, and largely funded by the Region.

### Open to the public on 28 and 29 November 2026

According to its website, Kanal opens to the public on the weekend of 28 and 29 November 2026, with ten exhibitions devoted to art and architecture. Attendance forecasts differ between sources. According to a STIB press release of Tuesday 24 November 2026, more than 30,000 visitors are expected over this weekend, which coincides with the first weekend of Plaisirs d'Hiver, Black Friday and a Hangar night at the Gare Maritime. According to BRUZZ of Thursday 26 November 2026, the museum expects 60,000 to 100,000 visitors for this free opening weekend. According to The Art Newspaper of 23 September 2026, quoting the museum, the 30,000 free tickets offered on reservation were all taken on the first day they went on sale.

Over the year, Kanal aims for about 550,000 visitors according to BRUZZ; La Libre of 24 November 2026 speaks of attendance estimated at between 500,000 and 600,000 visitors a year.

For all the events of the weekend, STIB is adding trains on metro lines 2 and 6 on Saturday evening only, every 8 minutes from 8 p.m. until the end of service, meaning every 4 minutes on the shared section between Simonis and Elisabeth, and on night line N18, with a bus every 20 minutes instead of every 30 between midnight and 3 a.m. The museum is served by the Yser stop (metro 2 and 6, bus 46); from 19 December 2026, tram 51 will again run via Yser and Sainctelette.

### Cost of the works and regional funding

The cost figures do not match. A Kanal spokesperson, quoted by The Art Newspaper on 29 January 2026, expected the total construction budget to reach 230 million euros. According to BRUZZ of Thursday 26 November 2026, Kanal's interim director puts the final cost of renovating and fitting out the former garage at 236 million euros, largely funded by the Region; the 2017 estimate was 150 million euros and the reference budget, equipment included, 163 million, and the director attributes 45 million to indexation and inflation. Another BRUZZ article of the same day gives an expected final bill of 238 million euros, 44 million of it due to inflation according to Kanal; the two articles do not explain the gap. According to La Libre of Wednesday 25 November 2026, the Region has spent nearly 300 million euros since 2015 on buying and converting the garage and launching the museum. The sources consulted do not say how these amounts relate to one another.

On Thursday 7 May 2026, the Brussels government granted Kanal a loan of 60 million euros to cover the final works, according to BRUZZ of 8 May 2026 (see the [Economy](/en/domains/economy) card). According to BRUZZ of 26 November 2026, Kanal borrowed 60 million euros from the Region in May 2026 to secure the opening. According to La Libre of 25 November 2026, the majority's compromise on the reduction of the allocation includes a loan of 60 million to complete the works.

The regional operating allocation is 28 million euros in 2026 and is set to fall towards 10 million euros a year in 2029, according to a Kanal spokesperson quoted by The Art Newspaper on 23 September 2026 and according to La Libre of 25 November 2026; the spokesperson added that the latter amount will only apply after discussion within the government. According to La Libre of 25 November 2026, the director general had proposed in May 2025 to reduce the annual allocation envisaged in the long term from 35 to 29 million euros. Another La Libre article, of 24 November 2026, speaks of an annual budget of 35 million euros in regional subsidies.

The partnership with the Centre Pompidou, concluded in late 2017, costs Kanal nearly 2 million euros a year from the opening according to La Libre, and 2 million a year until 2031 according to The Art Newspaper.

### Operations: closed to the public two days a week

According to BRUZZ of 26 November 2026, the building's management and maintenance budget, initially set at 13 million euros a year, has fallen below 10 million because of savings; nearly half of it, 4.5 to 5 million a year, goes on security, according to the interim director. To cut costs, Kanal will stay closed to the public two days a week, devoted primarily to private events. According to BRUZZ, Kanal received 28 million euros in 2026.

Source: [BRUZZ, Kanal in ten figures (26 November 2026)](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/cultuurnieuws/van-garage-tot-museum-kanal-tien-cijfers-2026-11-26). Confidence: *unconfirmed* (figures given by Kanal's management to a single publisher).

### Leadership: departure of the director general, interim and recruitment

At the Brussels cabinet meeting of **17 September 2026**, the Minister-President demanded the immediate dismissal of the Kanal Foundation's director general. The Deputy Minister-President opposed the move, citing legal and operational risks, and blocked the decision. According to press reports, the director general was reportedly offered an ultimatum: an immediate departure, or staying on with reduced powers until the museum's opening. The meeting was held behind closed doors and no statement was published; the accounts of BRUZZ and La Libre rest on anonymous government sources.

On **Monday 21 September 2026**, the Minister-President's office announced an agreement with the director general: his assignment ends on **30 November 2026**, two days after the museum's inauguration. According to the statement, the parties note "a divergence of views on the future of KANAL" and have agreed "to clarify the terms and timetable for the end of his assignment, which he had already announced". The director general remains in office until that date, but does not take part in the selection process for his successor. The statement does not mention the ultimatum reported by the press after the cabinet meeting of 17 September. According to The Art Newspaper, he had announced in early May 2026 that he would leave at the end of his second five-year term, staying in post until a successor was appointed, expected in early 2027. RTBF reports that the museum's leadership had caused sharp tensions within the regional majority in the days before the announcement.

According to BRUZZ of Tuesday 17 November 2026, 74 people applied for the post of director general of the Kanal Foundation; the jury shortlisted eight, who are interviewed from 17 November. Half of them will then go through to a third round, with an external assessment, before the decision of the jury and the board of directors. The jury has six members: three representatives of the board and three outside experts, including the chief executive of finance.brussels. The two government commissioners attend the interviews without being members of the jury and without voting rights. The appointment is planned for the week of 14 December 2026: according to BRUZZ, Kanal will therefore be without a director general for at least six weeks, and the artistic director and the financial director will act as interim. According to BRUZZ of 26 November 2026, the interim director forms the interim general management with the artistic director from 1 December 2026.

### Kanal Architecture: CIVA's move suspended

The move of CIVA, the Brussels centre for architecture and landscape renamed Kanal Architecture, into the Kanal building is suspended, according to BRUZZ of Friday 20 November 2026. The Minister-President's office confirms this, specifies that the decision was taken at the cabinet meeting of 16 July 2026 and says several options are still being examined. According to BRUZZ, the Budget Minister told De Standaard that CIVA might not move and could stay in Ixelles. The management contract concluded in 2023 between the Kanal Foundation and the government provided for CIVA's integration into Kanal.

For Kanal's opening, the library reading room, the Salon and the Kanal Architecture opening exhibition, "Right to the city - Right to the future", are ready. The archive and documentation centre cannot open: according to BRUZZ, the roof fire of summer 2026 damaged its installations, including the climate control. The archive, most of the books and most of the staff remain in Ixelles.

Source: [BRUZZ (20 November 2026)](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/cultuurnieuws/verhuizing-architectuurcentrum-civa-naar-kanal-staat-week-voor-opening-plots-hold-2026-11-20). Confidence: *unconfirmed* (a single outlet, reporting the office's confirmation; the decision of 16 July 2026 was not consulted).

### Building safety: July fire, tests and inspection

On **6 July 2026** at around 6 p.m., a fire broke out in a technical room under the roof of the construction site, affecting around **400 m²** of the "Kanal Architecture" building, intended for archives and collections. Firefighters brought the blaze under control by around 9 p.m., with no injuries. The cause is presumed accidental; a police and prosecutor's investigation was opened and a structural assessment of the roof scheduled. The Kanal Foundation's director general confirmed that the general opening remained set for 28 November 2026, adding that the "Kanal Architecture" building would not be ready for the inauguration.

On **28 September 2026**, according to Apache, quoted by BRUZZ, **five of the twelve safety tests** carried out the previous week (including fire safety, access control and electrics) had not been conclusive. The Brussels fire service (SIAMU) announced an inspection for Thursday 8 October 2026, stating that its opinion is advisory and that the decision on the opening rests with the mayor. According to the spokesperson for the Brussels fire service, quoted by BRUZZ on 8 October, the inspection took place on Wednesday 7 October 2026: all the tests were assessed positively and a favourable opinion was to be delivered to the City of Brussels.

Sources: [BX1](https://bx1.be/categories/news/un-incendie-sest-declare-sur-le-chantier-du-musee-kanal/) (6 July 2026); [La Libre](https://www.lalibre.be/regions/bruxelles/2026/07/06/incendie-au-musee-kanal-intervention-des-pompiers-en-cours-ZOLTPCQPFJAKTJSD5LHORCG6UM/) (6 July 2026); [The Art Newspaper](https://www.artnewspaper.fr/2026/07/08/le-futur-musee-kanal-pompidou-a-bruxelles-endommage-par-un-incendie) (8 July 2026); [BRUZZ](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/cultuurnieuws/kanal-slaagde-nog-niet-voor-brandveiligheid-op-8-oktober-nieuwe-test-2026-09-28) (28 September 2026); [BRUZZ](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/cultuurnieuws/brandweer-brussel-geeft-positief-veiligheidsadvies-voor-opening-kanal-2026-10-08) (8 October 2026). ## Flemish Community: savings on Dutch-language culture in Brussels

On Wednesday 30 September 2026, the Flemish government announced, with its 2027 budget, a series of subsidy cuts affecting Brussels institutions and municipalities. These decisions fall under the Flemish Community, not the Brussels Region.

### Bozar loses its Flemish subsidies from 2027 (1 October 2026)

The **Centre for Fine Arts (Bozar)**, a federal cultural institution based in Brussels, will no longer receive subsidies from the Flemish Community from **2027**. The news, revealed by VRT on **Thursday 1 October 2026**, was confirmed to BRUZZ. The amount is **€760,000**, or just under 2% of the operating budget, according to BRUZZ. According to Bozar, these subsidies accounted for **1.84%** of its 2025 revenue, which stood at **€38 million**, 52% of which came from the federal allocation and the National Lottery.

Bozar's director general says he was not consulted. According to him, the federal allocation does not cover staff and operating costs, and it is the other subsidies that fund the programming. He also states that the subsidy from the Brussels Region has fallen and stands at **€150,000 a year**. The Flemish Minister of Culture calls the withdrawal "very regrettable".

Sources: [BRUZZ](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/cultuurnieuws/bozar-verliest-alle-vlaamse-subsidies-2026-10-01) (1 October 2026); [La Libre](https://www.lalibre.be/belgique/2026/10/01/la-flandre-supprime-le-subside-quelle-accordait-a-bozar-GK472MV24NBIRD3TVAZJKHQMQI/) (1 October 2026); [Bozar, "Financial Model"](https://www.bozar.be/en/financial-model), accessed 2 October 2026. Confidence: *unconfirmed*. The information rests on two newsrooms (BRUZZ and La Libre); neither the Flemish decision nor Bozar's accounts were consulted, and the amount of the regional subsidy is a statement by the director general.

### Municipal culture and libraries: −30%, Screen Flanders abolished (2 October 2026)

From **2027**, the Flemish government is cutting by **30%** the operating subsidies for the Dutch-language cultural policy of the Brussels municipalities and for their Dutch-language libraries. The spokesperson for the Flemish Minister of Culture confirmed this to BRUZZ on **Friday 2 October 2026**: the funds provided for the Brussels municipalities under the Flemish decree on supralocal culture (Bovenlokaal Cultuurdecreet) fall from **€3.7 million to €2.6 million**. The Brussels aldermen concerned were informed by email. The Flemish minister responsible for Brussels says she is in discussions to see whether and how this saving can be offset. The Flemish Community Commission (VGC), for its part, puts the effect of the Flemish savings on its own funds in 2027 at about one million euros (see the [Institutional](/en/domains/institutional) card).

In the same series of savings, the economic fund **Screen Flanders** is abolished from **1 January 2027**: its budget of **€3.5 million** is withdrawn in full, the Vlaams Audiovisueel Fonds (VAF) confirms to BRUZZ. Created in 2012, the fund supports audiovisual productions on condition that the amount granted is spent in the Flemish Region; all Belgian producers are eligible. Brussels films and series have drawn on it, according to BRUZZ. The VAF says it hopes the decision will be reviewed.

Sources: [BRUZZ, on the municipal subsidies](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/politiek/vlaanderen-bespaart-dertig-procent-op-gemeentelijk-cultuurbeleid-en-bibliotheken-2026-10-02) (2 October 2026); [BRUZZ, on Screen Flanders](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/economie/screen-flanders-opgedoekt-ook-brusselse-films-en-series-verliezen-financieringsbron-2026-10-02) (2 October 2026). Confidence: *estimated* (single press source; figures confirmed to BRUZZ by the minister's spokesperson and by the VAF).

### Dutch-speaking libraries: a partial compensation challenged (30 October 2026)

According to BRUZZ, **600,000 euros**, including 400,000 from Flanders and 200,000 from the VGC, offset part of the Flemish saving of 1.1 million euros on Dutch-speaking libraries. At the VGC council on Friday 30 October 2026, Groen, a member of the majority, challenged the use of VGC money as long as the Flemish budget has not been voted.

Source: [BRUZZ (30 October 2026)](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/politiek/groen-noemt-oplossing-voor-besparing-bibliotheken-een-dwazigheid-2026-10-30). Confidence: *estimated*.

### Flemish archives in Brussels: the AMVB loses half of its Flemish subsidy (20 November 2026)

The AMVB (Archief en Museum voor het Vlaams leven te Brussel) keeps the archives of Flemish associations in Brussels, of cultural organisations such as the Kaaitheater, the KVS and the Beursschouwburg, and of Brussels artists. In early October 2026, the Flemish Minister of Culture announced that its Flemish subsidy would be halved. According to BRUZZ of Monday 23 November 2026, the organisation learned on Friday 20 November that the Flemish government is applying this reduction definitively, with no transitional measure.

The amounts given by BRUZZ differ from one article to the next. On 1 October 2026, the director spoke of about 250,000 euros less, and of a subsidy of 247,000 euros received separately from the VGC. On 23 November 2026, BRUZZ writes that 260,000 euros were to be cut and that the AMVB had until then received 521,000 euros from Flanders and 236,000 euros from the VGC; according to the director, the organisation loses a third of its budget on 1 January 2027, which falls from 750,000 to 500,000 euros. The articles do not explain these gaps.

The organisation has terminated its lease on the quai aux Pierres de Taille, considered too expensive: it pays there, among other things, 28,800 euros a year in office tax to the City of Brussels. It is looking for a building of about 1,500 m² and is changing its name to Archiefstuk. According to its director, its exhibitions are under threat.

Sources: [BRUZZ (1 October 2026)](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/cultuurnieuws/archief-en-museum-voor-vlaams-leven-te-brussel-verliest-helft-vlaamse-subsidies-bitter-2026-10-01); [BRUZZ (23 November 2026)](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/samenleving/archiefinstelling-amvb-verliest-helft-van-vlaamse-subsidies-expos-gevaar-2026-11-23). Confidence: *unconfirmed* (a single newsroom, quoting the director; neither the Flemish government's decision nor a second publisher was found).

## Wallonia-Brussels Federation: 12.9 million euros in cultural cuts (February 2026)

The FWB 2026 budget, adopted in late February, provides for **EUR 255 million in total savings**, including **EUR 12.9 million** in cuts to the cultural sector (approximately 4% of a cultural budget of EUR 320M+). Concrete measures:

- **Non-indexation** of cultural subsidies in 2026 (estimated saving: **EUR 3.8 million**)
- **Moratorium on new recognitions** and accreditations until 2028 (cultural centres, public libraries, visual arts, continuing education). Estimated non-spending: **~EUR 10M** in 2026, **~EUR 15M** in 2029
- Cumulative FWB savings reach **EUR 500 million over 4 years** (2026-2029)

Source: RTBF, "le budget 2026 de la FWB définitivement adopté", February 2026; FEAS, "commissions culture et budget", February 2026.

## Region: subsidies under review, visit.brussels and Zinneke Parade

### Coalition agreement of 12 February 2026

According to the press sources consulted in February 2026, the agreement of 12 February 2026 provides for:

- **Kanal**: a regional contribution for the museum's opening, of **EUR 60 million** according to La Libre and **EUR 50 million** according to La DH, two titles of the same group (see also the section "Cost of the works and regional funding" above);
- **Review of discretionary subsidies**: all regional subsidy envelopes will be re-examined as part of the fiscal consolidation effort of around EUR 1 billion.

According to Bozar's director general, the Brussels Region's subsidy to Bozar has fallen and stands at €150,000 a year (section "Bozar" above).

### visit.brussels: BD Comic Strip Festival cancelled (March 2026)

The 2026 edition of the **BD Comic Strip Festival**, scheduled for 25-27 September at the Gare Maritime (Tour & Taxis), has been **cancelled**. The direct cause: the **EUR 5.7 million** reduction in visit.brussels' 2026 budget, which falls from EUR 22 million to EUR 16.3 million. The CEO of visit.brussels confirmed the cancellation, adding that the **Iris Festival** would be reduced to a single concert on 9 May and that promotion of other events was frozen, except for Brussels Pride.

According to the announced budget trajectory, regional subsidies to visit.brussels would fall from **EUR 22 million to EUR 8 million by 2029**, which would threaten **90 of 160 jobs**.

Source: BX1 / Le Soir / BRUZZ (29-31 March 2026). Confidence: *estimated* (statements by the CEO of visit.brussels reported by several publishers; no visit.brussels document consulted).

### Zinneke Parade 2026

Ahead of the **Zinneke Parade 2026** (14th edition, **30 May 2026**, theme "Dream"), the regional subsidy was reduced from EUR 391,000 to **EUR 360,000** (35% of the total budget). The number of Zinnodes dropped from 20 to **13** due to insufficient funding. The Brussels Parliament adopted a resolution in December 2025 calling for financial guarantees; as of 2 March 2026, **no confirmation of structural funding** had been provided by the new government.

Source: L'Avenir, "Zinneke menacée", Oct 2025; BX1, "le Parlement veut lever l'incertitude", Dec 2025.

## City of Brussels: cultural budget down 19.6%

The City of Brussels reduced its cultural budget by **19.6%** between 2024 and 2026. Concrete consequences:

- **La Centrale** (contemporary art centre, 1,000 m²): **permanently closed on 22 February 2026** after 20 years of operation. 6 of 20 employees made redundant. The building is expected to "retain a cultural use" (undefined)
- **FAME Festival** (multidisciplinary feminist festival, founded 2021): funding **completely withdrawn** in February 2026, after 4 months of work on the 2026 edition
- Reduction in subsidies to the Theatre Royal du Parc and the KVS

Source: RTBF, "La Centrale ferme ses portes", 22 Feb 2026; L'Avenir, "le festival FAME disparaît", 19 Feb 2026.

According to the office of the City of Brussels alderwoman for Culture, quoted by BRUZZ on 13 October 2026, the call for a new cultural operator for the La Centrale site drew **seven applications** and the City selected none. It is reviewing the terms of the call, including the turnover ceiling of EUR 5.4 million, and will relaunch it at a date that has not been set. The handover of the site, planned for autumn 2026, will not happen within that timeframe.

Source: [BRUZZ (13 October 2026)](https://www.bruzz.be/select/expo/kunstencentrum-la-centrale-krijgt-nog-geen-opvolger-voorwaarden-worden-herbekeken-2026-10-13). Confidence: *estimated* (a single newsroom, quoting the alderwoman's office).

## Flagey: statutes without political office-holders (20 November 2026)

According to BRUZZ of Saturday 21 November 2026, the board of directors of the non-profit Flagey, the cultural venue in Ixelles, decided unanimously on Friday 20 November to set up a working group to prepare an amendment of its statutes. The aim is a future board from which people holding a political mandate would be excluded. According to its chair, the new statutes are to be approved by July 2027, followed by a call for candidates for the new board. Flagey hopes to conclude a new multi-year management agreement in autumn 2027 with the public authorities that fund it: the Brussels Region, the Wallonia-Brussels Federation, the municipality of Ixelles and the Flemish Community. Together they contribute just under EUR 3 million a year, about half of the non-profit's funding.

Since 2021, the management agreement has only been extended year by year. A five-year agreement could not be concluded because of a dispute over the composition of the board: two Vlaams Belang candidates have been refused since 2021, and Flemish rules on political representation on boards clash with the cordon sanitaire. According to BRUZZ, the Flemish Community's position on the plan remains unclear, although the funding authorities have reportedly already met.

Source: [BRUZZ (21 November 2026)](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/politiek/flagey-wil-raad-van-bestuur-depolitiseren-na-conflict-over-zitje-voor-vlaams-belang-2026-11-21). Confidence: *unconfirmed* (a single outlet; neither the board's decision nor a communication from Flagey was consulted).

## Alarm from the sector

**RAB/BKO** (Brussels Arts Network, 168 cultural actors) launched a public appeal for **financial guarantees** for 2026, denouncing an "unbearable uncertainty". Organisations have laid off staff, imposed part-time work or ceased their activities.

## Background: Dutch-speaking cultural policy (VGC)

Alongside the French-speaking policies (FWB, COCOF), the **VGC** (Vlaamse Gemeenschapscommissie) manages a structured Dutch-speaking cultural network in Brussels:

- **22 community centres** (gemeenschapscentra, N22 network): local venues for Dutch-speaking culture, youth and coexistence, spread across the 19 municipalities. The VGC is the governing authority ("inrichtende overheid")
- **19 local Dutch-speaking libraries**, one per municipality, supported by the VGC in content, management, funding and digital services
- **Muntpunt**: central Dutch-speaking cultural institution, located near the Grand-Place
- **18 municipal services** for Dutch-speaking culture

The VGC offers **arts subsidies** to organisations and individual artists in Brussels: operating subsidies (annual or three-year), project subsidies, trajectory subsidies and debut subsidies (maximum EUR 3,000 for first-time artists).

The **Paspartoe** programme (leisure pass) facilitates access to culture for disadvantaged groups, in collaboration with community centres and libraries.

Source: [VGC, "Lokaal cultuurbeleid"](https://www.vgc.be/), accessed 1 March 2026.
