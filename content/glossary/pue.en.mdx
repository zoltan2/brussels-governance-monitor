PUE is the ratio between the energy consumed by the whole facility and the energy consumed by servers, storage and network. Everything that is not computing, cooling, uninterruptible power supplies, lighting, conversion losses, inflates the numerator.

A PUE of **1.36** is the European weighted average measured across 776 data centres in 2026. In plain terms, for every 100 units of electricity destined for computing, the facility consumes 136.

The indicator has two known limits. It says nothing about the **absolute quantity** consumed: a very large efficient centre weighs more on the grid than a small inefficient one. And it says nothing about the **origin** of the electricity, which is why the EU label pairs it with separate indicators on the renewable share and waste heat reuse.
