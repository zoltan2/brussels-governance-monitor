## Social: poverty by municipality, families without shelter

According to Statbel, the five Belgian municipalities with the highest poverty risk are in Brussels: **32.8%** of Saint-Josse-ten-Noode residents lived below the national line in 2024, followed by Molenbeek-Saint-Jean (30.6%), Anderlecht (28%), Koekelberg (25.6%) and Schaerbeek (25.3%). Samusocial refused **3,396 people in families** in September and October 2026, out of 3,778 requests, according to RTBF and VRT NWS.

## Budget: the Court of Audit before Parliament

The Court of Audit puts the 2027 deficit at **EUR 735 million**, against a target of 719, BRUZZ reports. It also writes, according to the same outlet, that Article 23 allows all spending appropriations to be reallocated. The official tables of the adjusted 2026 budget put the ESA deficit at **EUR 956.5 million**.

## Mobility: taxis and shared bikes voted

On 20 November 2026 the Brussels Parliament adopted the ordinances on taxis and on shared mobility, according to Belga. From 1 January 2027, every new taxi must be zero-emission and shared e-scooters will disappear. According to BruxellesToday, quoting BX1, STIB must replace more than **1,292 wheels** on M7 metro trains from 2027.

## LEZ: no fines for foreign number plates

No LEZ fine targeted a foreign-registered vehicle in 2025 and 2026, against **17,717** for Belgian plates, according to the Brussels finance minister. Another count, reported by Belga, puts at 2,907 the fines of EUR 350 sent in 2026, as of 14 October.

## Overflights: the Region before the Council of State

The Region, the COCOM and the COCOF filed an appeal against the RNP 07L route on 16 November 2026. The Concertation Committee held the same day ended without agreement, according to Belga. According to Brussels Environment, the federal measure of 1 October has not reduced noise. Berchem-Sainte-Agathe is also going to the Council of State; Jette votes on 25 November 2026, BRUZZ reports.

## Housing: rental bans, socialised rents

**598** reminders from the regional inspectorate to landlords of homes under a rental ban went unanswered, BRUZZ reports. The Housing minister's office confirms to the same outlet that socialised rents will be aligned with those of social rental agencies.

## Employment and CPAS

Actiris will get no extra funding, confirms the employment minister, who estimates that nearly **10,000** more jobseekers will depend on the integration income by June 2027. The federal 2026 budget adjustment provides an extra EUR 210 million for the integration income, according to the federal minister quoted by RTBF; a delay in November payments remains possible.
