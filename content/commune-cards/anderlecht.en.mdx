## Biestebroeck marsh: The Dock project gets its permit (September 2026)

According to BX1, the planning permit for The Dock project, with more than 250 homes on the Biestebroeck marsh, was granted in September 2026. The municipality supports building on the site. The Alliance Marais Biestebroeck is challenging the decision in court and has gathered more than 3,300 signatures. The details are on the [Urban planning](/en/domains/urban-planning) card.

## Hôpitaux Universitaires de Bruxelles: the municipal council approves the grouping (15 October 2026)

On Thursday 15 October 2026, the Anderlecht municipal council approved the extension of the Hôpitaux Universitaires de Bruxelles grouping to CHU Saint-Pierre and the Hôpitaux Iris Sud, alongside Erasme, the Institut Jules Bordet and the Children's Hospital. The PTB and Team Fouad Ahidar abstained; the PTB cited the lack of time to discuss the plan with the trade unions. Ixelles and Saint-Gilles approved the plan the same evening, after the City of Brussels and Etterbeek on 12 October. Only the Université libre de Bruxelles still has to decide.

The grouping is planned for 1 January 2027. According to the figures reported by the press, it has 12 sites, 2,500 beds and a consolidated turnover of about EUR 1.5 billion. The plan's details are on the [City of Brussels](/en/municipalities/bruxelles-ville) page.

## Youth policy: a municipal building occupied for EUR 400 a year, "ambassadors" on hold (10 October 2026)

According to La DH of Saturday 10 October 2026, the Anderlecht municipal executive granted in **August 2025** the temporary occupation of a municipal building at Rue de Fiennes 75, in Cureghem, for **EUR 400 a year**, without a call for applications. The occupant is an association created in July 2025, which presents itself as a socio-educational support service for young people in difficulty. The alderwoman for Youth justifies the amount by the works the association has undertaken to carry out, and says a call for applications is not required for a temporary occupation.

The alderwoman says an accreditation application is under way with the Wallonia-Brussels Federation. Asked by the newspaper, the Federation replies that it received only a request for information in August 2025 and no file for this location; it says there is no accredited service of this type in Anderlecht and announces a follow-up of the case.

Also according to La DH, a private company created in March 2026 by a director of the association was registered at the building, with no amendment to the agreement and no rent. It moved its registered office after a question put to the municipal council in September 2026. The alderwoman says the municipality was not informed, speaks of a failing by the occupants and adds that the case is being examined.

The same article reports that the "youth ambassadors", presented to the municipal council in September 2026 as some twenty contact points in the neighbourhoods, do not exist to date. The alderwoman explains that about fifteen volunteers were trained in 2024, that the scheme was redirected towards associations and that the municipality is still prospecting.

Source: [La DH (10 October 2026)](https://www.dhnet.be/regions/bruxelles/2026/10/10/une-societe-privee-loges-et-des-ambassadeurs-fantomes-loges-dans-un-batiment-communal-pour-400-euros-par-an-on-netait-pas-au-courant-VJDB6QXSV5C2XDUJOKB5SJGST4/). ## Population: Anderlecht becomes the second municipality in the Region (1 January 2026)

On 1 January 2026, Anderlecht has **129,600 inhabitants**, 876 more than a year earlier (a rise of 0.7%), according to IBSA's Demographic Barometer 2026. It is the largest increase in number among the nineteen municipalities. Anderlecht overtakes Schaerbeek (129,278 inhabitants) and becomes the second most populated municipality in the Region, behind the City of Brussels.

Sources: [IBSA, Focus no. 85 (7 October 2026)](https://ibsa.brussels/sites/default/files/publication/documents/Focus-85_FRv3.pdf). Confidence: *official*.

## Place Grondels (Cureghem): works have started (5 October 2026)

Beliris and the municipality started the redesign of place Grondels on Monday 5 October 2026, at the junction of rue Grondels, rue Docteur Kuborn and rue Prévinaire, according to BRUZZ (Belga dispatch). The junction becomes a pedestrian zone open to cyclists and emergency services, with a playground, planting and a traffic filter; 22 parking spaces are removed.

The works cost 2.15 million euros and last around one year, in three phases. This is the first project delivered under a plan of eleven projects covering around 11 hectares around the Biestebroeck dock. The project is contested within the municipal executive.

Sources: [BRUZZ (5 October 2026)](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/mobiliteit/grondelsplein-kuregem-wordt-groene-voetgangerszone-2026-10-05). Confidence: *estimated* (agency dispatch, single source).

## Shots fired at the rue Démosthène police station (21 August 2026)

On the night of **Friday 21 to Saturday 22 August 2026**, shortly before midnight, two people who arrived **on an e-scooter** opened fire on the façade of the **police station on rue Démosthène**, then fled. Bullets went through the door that was targeted. The building was **closed** and **nobody was injured**.

It is the former central station of the **Midi police zone** (Anderlecht, Saint-Gilles, Forest), gradually emptied since the new station on rue des Deux Gares opened. A few teams are still based there and **the municipality wants to sell the building**.

Chief of police **Jurgen De Landsheer** points to reprisals following the summer's operations, which he says involved many arrests and seizures. The zone is reinforcing security around its stations and requesting support from the federal police. Mayor **Fabrice Cumps** (PS) says he is in close contact with the chief of police to strengthen protection measures for the teams as far as necessary. The federal interior minister condemned the attack. No arrests have been made at this stage.

Sources: [BX1 (22 August 2026)](https://bx1.be/categories/news/anderlecht-le-commissariat-de-la-rue-demosthene-vise-par-des-tirs-a-larme-lourde/) ; [BRUZZ (22 August 2026)](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/veiligheid/politiecommissariaat-anderlecht-beschoten-2026-08-22) ; [La Libre (22 August 2026)](https://www.lalibre.be/regions/bruxelles/2026/08/22/un-commissariat-a-anderlecht-cible-par-des-tirs-a-larme-lourde-une-pression-intolerable-ZZ5GRC3VPREOFIEOLJPW2HW3BU/). Confidence: official for the sequence of events, confirmed by the chief of police to several newsrooms; the reprisals hypothesis is a police reading, not an established fact.

## A large working-class municipality in digital transition

Anderlecht is the second most populated municipality in the Brussels Region, with nearly 130,000 inhabitants. Historically working-class and multicultural, it faces significant challenges in housing, employment, and social cohesion.

## Transparency: 4 out of 6 criteria

Anderlecht meets four of the six BGM monitoring criteria. The **budget** is published online, municipal council **minutes** are accessible, the **mandate registry** is available, and the municipality has a **citizen participation** platform (Decidim). However, it does not have its own **Open Data** portal, and the council **livestream** is classified as "partial" due to debates over its continuation.

## Citizen participation: Decidim

Anderlecht uses the **Decidim** platform (participez.anderlecht.be) to allow citizens to propose and debate municipal projects. Participatory budgets by neighbourhood are organised regularly.

## The livestream question

Anderlecht's municipal council introduced a livestream of its sessions, but this has been subject to debate regarding its continuation. Some elected officials have questioned the cost and usefulness of the system. BGM classifies this indicator as "partial" as long as the livestream is not guaranteed as a permanent practice.

## Budget 2026 and new taxes

Anderlecht's 2026 budget is presented as balanced and submitted for council vote on 5 March 2026. Among the new tax measures: a **tax on hookah tobacco outlets** and a **tax on fast-food restaurants**. The **resident parking card** increases from 15 to 30 EUR.

## CPAS under pressure

Anderlecht's CPAS (Public Centre for Social Welfare) faces growing pressure, with approximately 6,000 recipients of the social integration income (RIS). The CPAS budgetary situation is a major issue in municipal policy.

## Foyer anderlechtois: regional commission of inquiry (June 2026)

Following suspicions of clientelism in the allocation of social housing by the **Foyer anderlechtois** (public service real-estate company, SISP) — revealed by a Pano (VRT) report and followed by **searches** confirmed by the Brussels public prosecutor's office on 28 May 2026 — the Brussels Parliament approved, on **1 June 2026**, the creation of a **parliamentary commission of inquiry** in a short and intensive version, running until **21 July 2026**. On **2 June**, the Brussels Parliament's **ethics committee** postponed its review of the housing alderman's case, citing the ongoing criminal investigations, and Anderlecht's **municipal college** decided not to take any measure against him. The suspicions are not confirmed at this stage (presumption of innocence).

On **5 June 2026**, BRUZZ reports that Parliament will give the **formal start** to the committee the week of 8 June. The **MR** denounces the allocation of a Foyer building to **vzw Yamry** for **EUR 60/month**, an association linked to a former Foyer official implicated in the ongoing investigations.

The full institutional follow-up is on the [Institutional](/en/domains/institutional) card.

## Parking plan for Goede Luchtwijk / Bon Air (3 July 2026)

Following a **citizen consultation** on two scenarios, Anderlecht's **municipal college** approved, in early July, a plan to regularise parking in the **Goede Luchtwijk (Bon Air)** neighbourhood, where **363 of the 466 existing spaces** had been used illegally since a redevelopment of the road layout in 2023 (parking enforcement there had in fact been suspended in April 2025 for lack of an applicable framework).

The adopted plan provides for approximately **340 regulated parking spaces**, as well as turning the neighbourhood into a **zone de rencontre** (speed limited to 20 km/h, shared use of the road by pedestrians, cyclists and vehicles). Implementation is targeted for **sometime in 2027**, subject to the opinions of emergency services and police.

## Public outdoor swimming pool project at the Abattoirs (Cureghem)

The **Brussels government**, at the initiative of the Secretary of State for Urban Renewal **Ans Persoons** (Vooruit), has launched a DBFM-type tender to build a **public outdoor swimming pool** (a covered 25 m pool + an outdoor 25 m pool) on the roof of the "Manufacture" building, on the **Abattoirs** site in Anderlecht (Cureghem neighbourhood). **This is a regional decision, not a municipal one.** The project targets a capacity of approximately **400,000 visitors per year** and more than **3,500 hours of opening per year** across 50 weeks; more than **EUR 4 million** has already been invested in reinforcing the roof, and heating would rely on heat recovery from the building.

## Major fire at avenue Maurice Herbette (7 June 2026)

On **7 June 2026**, a **major fire** broke out in a warehouse at **avenue Maurice Herbette** in Anderlecht. A **municipal emergency phase** was declared and then **lifted** after the fire was brought under control. **Heavy black smoke** spread over the neighbourhood. Residents were asked to keep **windows and doors closed** during the intervention.
