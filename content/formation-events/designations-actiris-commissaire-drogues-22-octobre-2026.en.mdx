## The fact

On **Thursday 22 October 2026**, the Brussels government appoints **Isabelle Grippa**, director of hub.brussels, as director-general of **Actiris**, and **Kristof Devuyst** as deputy director-general, according to La Libre, BRUZZ and BX1. Since the director-general resigned in March 2026, the leadership had been exercised under delegated authority. According to La Libre, the Employment minister announces a mission letter with **19 work streams**, each with its own timetable.

The same day, the government appoints the **regional commissioner for security and drugs**. She previously headed the Security and Prevention department of the City of Leuven. According to BRUZZ, 28 applications had been received.

## What remains open

The commissioner's start date has not been announced. Two opposition groups contest the selection procedure at Actiris and the gap between the jury's ranking and the commissioner's appointment. See the [Employment](/en/domains/employment) and [Security](/en/domains/security) cards.
