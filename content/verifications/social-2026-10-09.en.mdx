## Verification carried out on 9 October 2026

**Result: factual errors and attributions corrected.**

### Method

The sources cited by the card were opened one by one and each key figure was compared with the text of its source. About 57 items were checked.

### Corrections applied

- Social barometer: the poorest 20% of Brussels residents, not 2%, spend more than half of their income on housing.
- Unemployment exclusions: 35,799 people in the Brussels Region in 2026 (Brulocalis, ONEM figures); the figure of 8,000 concerned the municipality of the City of Brussels.
- Cost for the CPAS: EUR 145.8 million per year for the Brussels CPAS (Brulocalis); the billion concerned the Walloon CPAS.
- Shelter refusals: 127 per week, a figure cited by the Flemish Children's Rights Commissioner's office.
- Kanal: EUR 50 million according to La DH, 60 million according to La Libre.
- Commissioner for security and drugs: post provided for in the agreement, not yet filled.
- Stations Plan: EUR 3 million in 2026 for the first phase.
- Population: 1,255,834 inhabitants on 1 January 2026 (IBSA).
- Removed for lack of a source: unexpected expense, in-work poverty, number of women in the eighth count, the statement that social services are protected, an older figure for the number of children receiving family allowances.

### Limits

One figure could not be verified and is left unchanged: the EUR 33 million in savings on family allowances (subscriber-only Le Soir article). The body of the card beyond the first three sections and BX1 sources were not reread.
