## The largest municipality by area

Uccle is the largest municipality in the Brussels Region by area (22.9 km²) and the fifth by population (nearly 87,000 inhabitants). A residential and green municipality, it is governed by a renewed MR-DéFI + Ecolo-Groen coalition.

## Transparency: 5 out of 6 criteria

Uccle achieves a very good score. The **budget** and council **minutes** (with dedicated archives) are published online, the council is broadcast via **livestream** on YouTube, the **mandate registry** is accessible, and the municipality runs a **participatory budget** of EUR 150,000 via the MonOpinion platform. Only the **Open Data** portal is missing.

## Citizen participation: MonOpinion

Uccle uses the **MonOpinion** platform (based on Decidim) for its participatory budget of **EUR 150,000**, allowing citizens to propose and vote on improvement projects for the municipality.

## Recent developments

The **property tax** (PRI) has been raised from 2,940 to **3,400 additional centimes**. The **PFAS** contamination at the Sicli site (Chaussee de Neerstalle 430) remains a major issue: a 100-metre safety perimeter has been in place since September 2025 (see PFAS dossier). A collaboration project with the municipality of **Linkebeek** is under consideration.

## Tram 82 extended to the Dieweg (7 June 2026)

From **Saturday 7 June 2026**, tram 82 runs again via **rue de Stalle** and **square Marlow** and is **extended to the Dieweg**, following works on rue de l'Etoile. Uccle is now directly connected to **Brussels-Midi station**.

Source: [BX1 (7 June 2026)](https://bx1.be/categories/news/uccle-le-tram-82-est-desormais-prolonge-jusquau-dieweg/).

## CetteFamille — first shared housing for elderly in Belgium (June 2026)

The first **shared housing for elderly people in Belgium** has opened in Uccle. This model, developed by **CetteFamille**, combines autonomy with home care support, as an alternative to traditional care homes. **Iriscare** management visited the facility, signalling institutional interest in this model within the Region's elderly care policy.

Source: [Iriscare (3 June 2026)](https://www.iriscare.brussels/fr/2026/06/03/cettefamille-simplante-en-belgique-iriscare-a-visite-la-premiere-maison-a-uccle/).

## Fedasil reception centre on rue Beeckman: opening suspended by the courts (12 July 2026)

The transfer to the Samusocial/Fedasil reception centre with 230 places on rue Beeckman, whose opening had been announced for **14 July 2026**, was **provisionally suspended** by the Brussels court of first instance on **12 July 2026**, following an appeal by residents disputing the building's compliance with planning rules. No ruling on the merits was known as of **20 July 2026**.

The case triggered a contradictory citizen mobilisation: a petition supporting the centre gathered **1,376 signatures**, against **1,528** for the opposing petition, according to a count published in mid-July.

Sources: RTBF, La Libre (12 July 2026); DH, Sudinfo (15-16 July 2026). Confidence: official for the court suspension; both petitions are reported strictly neutrally.

## Parc Raspail reopens (10 July 2026)

Parc Raspail, a listed site closed for over a year for restoration works (boundary wall, ice house, pond, 19th-century features), reopened to the public on **10 July 2026**. An official inauguration is planned for **12 September 2026**.

Source: DH, Sudinfo (11 July 2026).

## 2025 municipal accounts in surplus

The municipal council of **25 June 2026** approved the 2025 accounts, closed with a **surplus of EUR 1.38 million** (overall result of EUR 14.19 million), despite an exceptional transfer of over EUR 5 million to the CPAS and EUR 17.6 million in pension charges.

Source: BX1 (June 2026).

## Rue Beeckman: transfer relaunched then re-suspended by the courts (3 August 2026)

On **Monday 3 August 2026**, the **Samusocial** launched the transfer of **238 applicants for international protection** from Koekelberg to the former **Armonea** building on rue Beeckman. The operation was halted **within twenty-four hours**: the **Brussels court of first instance** extended the suspension of the project, seized by **two resident collectives** contesting the building's planning compliance since the start of July.

The Samusocial stated that it respects the court decision and is suspending all installation operations until further notice. The **final ruling is expected in mid-August 2026**.

This new episode extends the provisional suspension of 12 July documented above. The number of people concerned, 238, is that of the transfer actually launched, to be distinguished from the centre's announced capacity of 230 places.

Sources: [La Libre (4 August 2026)](https://www.lalibre.be/belgique/2026/08/04/la-justice-stoppe-le-demenagement-du-centre-daccueil-du-samusocial-a-uccle-decision-attendue-a-la-mi-aout-NC7JJCRVOBEP7NYM2UGI5VK464/) ; [La DH (4 August 2026)](https://www.dhnet.be/regions/bruxelles/2026/08/04/la-justice-stoppe-le-demenagement-du-centre-daccueil-du-samusocial-a-uccle-linstallation-du-03-aout-etait-parfaitement-legale-YVMJCEXYNVETHAHAE656F5EBCI/). ## Reopening the Ukkelbeek to reduce flooding (August 2026)

The municipality began works this summer to **reopen the Ukkelbeek**, which rises on avenue De Fré, and connect it to the **pond in front of the castle in Wolvendael park**. Stated aim: reduce recurrent flooding in the lower part of **avenue De Fré** and improve the pond's biodiversity.

**The mechanism.** The pond will act as a **natural storm basin**: its overflow is regulated by a structure known as a "monk", which controls the water level towards a pipe joining the Ukkelbeek via rue Rouge, and temporarily stores part of the runoff from the park and the Crabbegat path. The connection should also oxygenate the pond; the shrub layer, judged to be in poor condition and encroaching on the reed bed, will be replaced by aquatic plants.

**The works.** The stretch of avenue De Fré between avenue du Manoir and the park entrance has been closed since early August 2026, with a diversion via avenue de l'Echevinage and avenue Brugmann, and no parking in the works zone. The park's main entrance may be closed if needed, with access maintained through the small gate along the castle. Works due to end on **24 August 2026**.

The municipality also launched a study in **January 2026** on reopening the **Linkebeek**, which disappeared from the landscape in the 1960s.

Source: [BRUZZ (10 August 2026)](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/stedenbouw/openleggen-van-de-ukkelbeek-moet-overstromingen-tegengaan-2026-08-10). Confidence: unconfirmed.
