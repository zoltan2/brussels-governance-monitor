Social · Municipalities · 01 / 06

Saint-Josse has the lowest median income in Belgium.

21,891 euros a year in 2024, adjusted for household composition. The five municipalities with the country's highest poverty risk are in Brussels.

On Thursday 19 November 2026 Statbel published residents' disposable income by municipality for 2024. Saint-Josse-ten-Noode has the lowest median in the country, followed by Molenbeek-Saint-Jean (22,220 euros) and Anderlecht (22,931 euros). The highest is in Attert, in the province of Luxembourg: 45,790 euros.

Official figure

median annual disposable income in Saint-Josse-ten-Noode in 2024

  • 32.8% of Saint-Josse residents below the national poverty line
  • Then Molenbeek 30.6%, Anderlecht 28%, Koekelberg 25.6%, Schaerbeek 25.3%

The nuance that matters

An adjusted income, not a salary

The calculation adds up the income of the whole household, benefits included, then adjusts it for household composition. The amount is therefore neither a salary nor a household's total budget. Statbel specifies that these municipal figures do not replace the regional and national poverty rates from its European income survey.

02

Budget · Region · 02 / 06

2027 budget: the Court of Audit sees a deficit of 735 million.

The government is aiming for 719 million euros. The Court presented its report to Parliament on 19 November 2026, according to the press.

According to the press, the Court of Audit finds the Region's budget path improving, but puts the 2027 deficit at 735 million euros. It urges caution over about one billion euros of investment kept outside the balance over the legislature, and notes underestimated appropriations, notably at Actiris and STIB.

03

Social · COCOM · 03 / 06

Emergency shelter: 3,396 refusals for people in families.

In September and October 2026 Samusocial received 3,778 requests from people in families and refused 3,396 of them for lack of space.

Samusocial says it has never had so many places for families, yet refuses the great majority of requests. According to the operator, stays are getting longer: files are hard to open at the CPAS, follow-on facilities are full and the private rental market is almost out of reach for low incomes.

The figures come from the operator

They were reported by two newsrooms, and the Samusocial statement could not be found on 19 November 2026. Emergency shelter for homeless people falls under the COCOM, the Common Community Commission. The reception of asylum seekers is a federal competence.

04

Housing · Region · 04 / 06

Homes banned from letting: 598 reminders unanswered.

The regional inspectorate sent 1,115 reminders to landlords of homes placed under a rental ban between 2020 and 2024.

In a written answer to Parliament, published on 10 November 2026 and reported on the 15th, the Secretary of State for Housing states that 598 of the 1,115 reminders went unanswered. They concerned rental bans still in force, issued because the home does not meet safety and health requirements.

Official figure

598

reminders from the regional housing inspectorate went unanswered

  • 170 registrations of residents in banned homes reported by municipalities
  • 17 landlords fined in 2025, including three repeat offenders

The nuance that matters

Unanswered does not mean let again

The answer says it “cannot be ruled out” that these homes were still let, without establishing that they were. Of the owners who replied, most say they live in the home themselves and several say they were never told about the ban. The period covered by the 170 registrations is not specified.

05

Mobility · Region · 05 / 06

Low emission zone: not a single fine for foreign number plates.

In 2025 and in 2026, up to 23 October, 17,717 fines targeted vehicles registered in Belgium.

The Brussels finance minister states this in answer to a written question. The cameras read foreign plates, but Brussels Fiscality has no access to other countries' registration data: without the owner and the vehicle's Euro standard, no fine can follow.

  • 9,561 fines for Belgian plates in 2025

The nuance that matters

Agreements exist in Antwerp and Ghent

Both cities fine foreign drivers thanks to agreements with France and the Netherlands; the minister considers that these bring in little. The 17,717 fines should not be confused with the 2,907 fines also published this week, a different count covering vehicles banned since 1 January 2026.

06

Mobility · Region · 06 / 06

Taxis: zero emission is voted for 1 January 2027.

The Brussels Parliament adopted the ordinance on 20 November 2026. On the same day it adopted the framework for shared bikes.

From 1 January 2027, every taxi newly registered in Brussels must be zero-emission. The text takes up the support measures announced by the government in October. On the same date shared e-scooters disappear, under the other ordinance adopted on 20 November.

The nuance that matters

The obligation applies to new registrations

Combustion taxis registered before 2027 may keep running for up to nine years, and the low emission zone continues to apply to them. Each vote is known through a single news agency dispatch: neither the adopted texts nor the record of the sitting were consulted for this issue.
