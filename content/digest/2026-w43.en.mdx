## Security: a permanent framework against drug trafficking

On Wednesday 21 October 2026, the Minister-President signed a police order that makes the framework for fighting drug trafficking permanent; until then it had been extended every six months. In the **19 sensitive zones**, electric scooters, shared or private, may no longer be ridden or parked. Across the whole Region, the order bans pyrotechnic devices and the sale of nitrous oxide, except to professionals.

On Thursday 22 October 2026, the government appointed the **regional commissioner for security and drugs**. During the youth demonstration on Saturday 24 October, the police preventively seized saline solution, masks and goggles; according to the Ligue des droits humains, these seizures are illegal.

## Employment: Actiris has its management and its roadmap

On 21 October 2026, Actiris published its roadmap towards **70%** employment in 2030: around **57,000** more people from Brussels in work, of whom nearly 9,000 are expected to come from Actiris's own levers. Every new registrant will be contacted no later than **15 days** after registering. On 22 October, the government appointed Isabelle Grippa as director-general and Kristof Devuyst as deputy.

## Housing: the Region ordered to act for Travellers

In a judgment of 15 October 2026, the French-speaking court of first instance of Brussels ordered the Region to open sites for at least **60 caravans**, within six months of service of the judgment. Penalty payments are capped at **500,000 euros**. The government is analysing the judgment before deciding on a possible appeal.

**See the [housing card →](/en/domains/housing)**

## Budget: the property tax reduction for children stays a percentage

According to BRUZZ, the government abandoned at second reading, on 22 October 2026, the flat amount planned for the property tax reduction for children: it remains **10%** per child. The doubling of the Be Home bonus, from 164 to **328 euros**, was approved the same day. The 9 million euros in savings planned for 2027 disappear. According to La Libre, the council of ministers of 22 October also reached an agreement on the budget, with no figures published.

**See the [budget card →](/en/domains/budget)**

## Urban planning: the Canal Plan's record

According to perspective.brussels, around **4,500 homes** were authorised in the Canal Area between 2015 and 2024, and the area gained around 9,000 jobs between 2015 and 2023. The Council of State annulled the permit for the Key West project, at the Biestebroeck basin, in Anderlecht ([Anderlecht card](/en/municipalities/anderlecht)). According to BRUZZ, Parliament reactivated the Regional Development Commission on 16 October, and the Region will not issue a subdivision permit for the Josaphat site before a government decision.

**See the [urban planning card →](/en/domains/urban-planning)**

## Mobility: fewer tram accidents

According to the STIB, accidents involving a tram fell from **1,196** in 2023 to **889** in 2025, around a quarter fewer. According to BX1, Parliament's Mobility committee heard in October 2026 the authors of a petition against the withdrawal of the STIB's 12 minibuses reserved for people with reduced mobility ([accessibility dossier](/en/dossiers/accessibilite-pmr)).

## Economy: more companies leave than arrive

According to Statbel, **1,687** Brussels companies moved to Flanders in 2025, and 1,263 Flemish companies made the opposite move. With Wallonia, Brussels recorded 1,139 departures for 804 arrivals. According to BRUZZ, the Region is abolishing Brucefo, its food expertise centre.
