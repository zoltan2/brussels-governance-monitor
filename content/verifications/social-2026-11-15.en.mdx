## Verification carried out on 15 November 2026

**Result: four key figures updated or clarified, no wrong figure.**

### Method

The sources of the card's 29 key figures, of its summary and of its two status boxes were opened one by one, and each number was compared with the text of its source. Twenty-five pages and documents were read.

### Corrections applied

- Regional commissioner for security and drugs: the card described her as "not yet appointed" (situation on 29 August 2026). Her appointment is reported by BRUZZ on 23 October 2026.
- Ninth count of people without a home: it is no longer the "next" one. According to La DH of 14 November 2026, the count was held on the night of 15 to 16 October 2026 and its results had not been published at that date. The summary and the box "What this means in practice" are updated.
- Homeless minors: the 1,678 minors come from the eighth count. The "more than 1,000 children who regularly sleep rough" are a separate estimate by the Flemish children's rights commissioner and the Délégué général aux droits de l'enfant, not a share of the 1,678.
- Samusocial places for single men: the press release of 30 September 2026 gives 240 places and the loss of 130 places; the figure of 370 places before comes from BRUZZ. The attribution is clarified.

### Confirmed

Water poverty, social supplement to family allowances, COCOM budget and vote (46 yes, 24 no, 1 abstention for the expenditure budget), Social Barometer (poverty, integration income, social housing, undocumented people, children, poorest 20%), students on integration income, 325 secured places, eighth count, shelter refusals, population, City of Brussels CPAS, cost and exclusions of the unemployment reform (Brulocalis), Stations Plan, Kanal, disability, Humanitarian Hub, resources announced in the 2027 budget.

### Limits

Three figures could not be reread and are kept as they are: the 56,497 recipients of integration income (the dashboard of the federal public service for social integration cannot be read without a browser), the 33.6% rate of risk of poverty or social exclusion and the 28.8% rate in 2023 (not found on the Statbel page cited), and the EUR 33 million in savings on family allowances (Le Soir article reserved for subscribers). The body of the card beyond the sections cited by the boxes and the BX1 sources were not reread.
