## The fact

On **Friday 23 October 2026**, the Brussels government approves the outline of the reform of the **22 social rental agencies** (AIS). They let more than **8,100 homes** from private owners to low-income households. Entry into force is planned for **early 2028**.

## The eight pillars

A financial barometer; graduated supervision; a common governance framework; ex-post checks on allocations; stronger checks on housing quality; a single registration list for participating agencies; a gradual reorganisation, with a minimum number of homes per agency; a growth roadmap for the housing stock.

## What remains open

Each agency would be assessed every year on its financial health. In case of difficulty, a step-by-step recovery plan would come before any withdrawal of accreditation. The press release cites the bankruptcies of the Saint-Josse and Anderlecht-Cureghem agencies in 2025. See the [Housing](/en/domains/housing) card.
