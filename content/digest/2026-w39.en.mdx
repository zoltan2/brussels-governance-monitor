## Budget : Rise in Non-Taxable Income

According to an analysis by BISA for BRUZZ, **43 %** of the 791,621 Brussels personal income tax returns for 2023 revenues resulted in no tax, compared to **35 %** in 2014. This increase reflects growing poverty in the Region. The regional tourist accommodation tax rose from **4 to 5 euros** per night at the standard rate as of 2026.
