## The fact

On **Monday 31 August 2026** the **Council of State suspended** the decision by which the City of Brussels had awarded, on **17 July 2026**, the **Brussels casino concession** to **ECK**, the company operating Napoleon Games. The appeal came from the **outgoing operator**, in place since 2010.

## The grounds

The court holds that the reasoning does not allow verification that all elements of the tender specifications were taken into account, that the assessment of certain partnerships relied on letters of recommendation rather than firm commitments, and that the obligations of the European regulation on foreign subsidies distorting the internal market were not respected.

## The amounts at stake

As reported in the press: a **fifteen-year** concession, some **750 million euros**, with operation due to start on **1 January 2027**. The outgoing operator pays the City **4.7 million euros** a year, the winning bid promised **10.075 million**.

## What followed at municipal level

On **Monday 7 September 2026** the municipal council questioned the mayor, who announced that he was considering **standing aside** from casino files in the college while denying any conflict of interest. A notification of an alleged breach of the City's code of ethics, adopted on 29 June 2026 and filed on 24 August, targets his seat on the board of a club of which the selected concession holder is a main sponsor.

**BGM takes no position on whether a conflict of interest exists**: that is the subject of a separate procedure. And a suspension is not an annulment.
