## Corrections and Employment Domain Updates

The week focused mainly on statistical corrections and adjustments following data released by Actiris in July and August. The number of Brussels job seekers registered with a CPAS in June 2026 has been corrected: the figure of **31,850 people** (representing **34 %** of the total) and a year-on-year increase of **70.6 %** are now retained, replacing two figures initially in circulation. This correction reflects harmonised Actiris data from 30 July and remains consistent with the announced proportion.

By the end of July 2026, Actiris counted **97,392 registered job seekers**, representing **3.4 %** more than a year earlier. The administrative unemployment rate stands at **15.7 %**. Two contradictory movements characterise the month: the number of people receiving unemployment benefits fell by **40.1 %** (approximately **20,848 fewer people**), while **33,075 people** (or **34 %** of the total) are now registered with a CPAS, a proportion rising by **70.6 %** year-on-year. Those under 25 increased by **17 %** and young people in job-insertion schemes by **30.2 %**.

This gradual shift from federal unemployment insurance to CPAS systems, funded by municipalities and the Cocom, continues and concentrates local budget pressures.

## Security: Duty-Related Death at Emergency Services

On Friday, 14 August 2026, two explosions at Treurenberg 22 (Single Resolution Board building) claimed the life of Sergeant-Major **Olivier Francotte**, firefighter from Brussels's 21st company. A second firefighter was seriously injured, along with two Sibelga employees. A contractor installing fibre-optic cable had drilled into a low-pressure gas line. Approximately one hundred people were evacuated before the explosions. The Labour Auditorate has been notified.

This is the first documented on-duty death on the security map. The incident occurs three weeks after suspension of a five-day strike over SIAMU resources, with the strike notice remaining in effect until end-September 2026.

## Mobility: Smoke and Metro Service Interruptions

Three smoke incidents interrupted metro lines 2 and 6 between Wednesday 12 and Thursday 13 August 2026. The first two stemmed from external sleepers that overheated near Clemenceau and Delacroix stations. The third, Thursday around 18:20, came from beneath a train and led to evacuation of Porte de Hal station. STIB emphasises that the three events are distinct and isolated, with heat as a possible explanation for the two overheating incidents. No direct link has been established at this stage. No injuries reported.

## Additional Domains

**Social:** Iriscare announced on 29 July the payment in August of the annual age supplement to over **300,000 Brussels children**, totalling more than **16 million euros**. Amounts range from **€25.36** for children under 6 to **€101.46** for 18-24-year-olds in higher education. Payment is automatic.

**Urban Planning:** Mini-Europe served notice on 10 August to the City of Brussels and SRL Neo not to commence the Heysel Sports Park project without mobility guarantees. The park contests the closure of access roads, reduction of parking from approximately **400 to 89 spaces**, and loss of the only coach parking. Works are scheduled for September 2026, supported by a regional loan potentially reaching **€50 million**.

**Economy:** The Region's last two Gamma stores closed this summer: Uccle (rue de Stalle, 1 July) and Anderlecht (rue du Cerf Court, August). Intergamma cites business reasons without further explanation.

## Municipalities: Works and Investments

**Uccle:** The municipality has undertaken summer works to uncover the Ukkelbeek and reconnect it to Wolvendael park's pond to reduce recurring flooding and improve biodiversity. The avenue De Fré section between avenue du Manoir and the park entrance has been closed since early August, with completion scheduled for 24 August 2026.

**Forest:** Jupiter Park (approximately 2.6 hectares) reopened its pathways on 8 June 2026 following works begun in September 2025. The neighbouring Duden Park construction began 3 August 2026 for one year across six phases. Beliris invests **€5.5 million** in both parks with the municipality and Bruxelles Environnement.

**Brussels City:** The City increases supplementary property tax from **2,950 to 3,457** for 2026, raising the municipal share from **36.875 %** to **43.2125 %** of indexed cadastral income. For owner-occupiers, an automatic municipal premium fully compensates this increase plus adds a **€100 flat amount**.

## Active Dossiers

**SLRB:** Four associations filed a summary judgment application on 13 July 2026 against SLRB to halt the sale of the Ariane (Woluwe-Saint-Lambert) and Palais (Schaerbeek) sites, purchased for developing hundreds of social housing units. The sale should yield approximately **€52 million** for the debt repayment plan. Applicants oppose this with **62,234 households** on the social housing waiting list. More than a month after filing, no judicial decision has been published.

**Brussels Airspace:** Evere municipality announced on 14 August 2026 joining the cessation action filed by the Brussels Region against the RNP-07L approach route. Evere thus joins Schaerbeek and Berchem-Sainte-Agathe. The Region contends that over **270,000 Brusselaars** suffer from this route and **96 %** of measured landings exceed Brussels noise standards.

**Vivaqua:** Facing drought and heat waves, Vivaqua activated water reserves in two quarries (Écaussinnes and Ligny) and requested Walloon and Flemish clients to limit consumption. Meuse flow at Tailfer is declining towards the **19 m³/s** threshold, below which Tailfer plant water production would be significantly curtailed.

**Shared Mobility:** The petition to preserve shared scooters in Brussels has exceeded the **1,000-signature threshold** requiring Parliament's engagement: **1,595 signatures** by 12 August, the most-signed democratie.brussels initiative. It targets the decision to ban such devices from 1 January 2027. The threshold guarantees public debate, does not oblige the majority to change position, and suspends nothing.

## Radar and Active Signals

Several active signals complement the institutional and socio-economic picture:

**Transport:** The shared-scooter question, already backed by a citizen movement with over **1,500 signatures**, could be examined by the Mobility Commission before the first plenary session scheduled for 21 September.

**Climate and Water:** The Regional Drought Cell maintains yellow alert since 23 July, recommending deferral of non-essential water use. Fifteen Walloon municipalities have adopted water-use restriction orders. No Brussels-specific restriction measures have been documented at this stage.

**Institutional:** The Parliament's Ethics Commission ruled in late April 2026 on a function accumulation case. The ruling was communicated neither to the complainant (a Saint-Josse-ten-Noode councillor) nor to the public. The institutional issue concerns the lack of transparency in this Commission's decisions.
