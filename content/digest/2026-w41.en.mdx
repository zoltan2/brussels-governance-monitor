## 2027 budget: an agreement, then an interrupted cabinet meeting

The Brussels government reached an agreement on the 2027 budget on 4 October 2026, with a target deficit of **719 million euros**. The return to balance remains set for 2029. The general policy statement was delivered on 5 October 2026 before the Brussels Parliament. According to La DH, a gap of **129 million euros** on 2026 had to be closed. The budget is due to be tabled in Parliament before 31 October 2026.

On 5 October 2026, the majority gave two versions on the **131 million euros** intended for the SLRB: confirmed according to the Socialist ministers and the Budget Minister, not dealt with according to the Minister-President. On 8 October 2026, the cabinet meeting was interrupted on this point; the Socialist ministers left the meeting.

## Employment: more than 100,000 registered jobseekers

**100,198** jobseekers were registered with Actiris at the end of September 2026, up **5.8%** over a year. The unemployment rate stands at **16.1%**.

Paid educational leave: **2.3 million euros** in subsidies have been blocked or reclaimed for fraud in Brussels, according to La DH and BruxellesToday. The Economy Minister stated this in committee on 6 October 2026. According to BruxellesToday, the Region had been alerted as early as July 2023.

The statement of 5 October 2026 announces an "effort" of **55 million euros** on employment policies in 2027 and the replacement of Activa by a new support measure. The target of a **70%** employment rate in 2030 is maintained.

## Demonstration of 9 October: arrests, minors released

The national demonstration of 9 October 2026 in Brussels led to around **110 arrests**, according to a provisional police count. **21 people** were brought before the public prosecutor's office, including **17 minors**. BX1 reports accounts of police violence.

On 11 October 2026, the prosecutor's office says it had requested eight placements of minors; for lack of places in institutions, they were released. **283** French-speaking **young people** are waiting for a place in a closed section.

According to BRUZZ, three trams and four buses of the STIB were damaged on the sidelines of the demonstration. Up to **98%** of white bags were not collected on 9 October 2026 according to the municipality, and four Recyparks remained closed. Catch-up rounds are announced from 12 October 2026.

## Climate: zero-interest loans and air at schools

Zero-interest loans replace the Renolution grants in spring 2027. The budget agreement of 4 October 2026 allocates **66 million euros** a year to them.

Separately, all Brussels schools exceed the WHO recommendation for nitrogen dioxide, according to a modelling study published on 8 October 2026 by Les Chercheurs d'air.

## Mobility: tunnels, road tax and road safety

The like-for-like renovation of the Bailli and Vleurgat tunnels has been announced, with an investment plan expected in November 2026. The statement of 5 October also announces a new Regional Mobility Plan by the end of 2028.

Small city cars and zero-emission vehicles are exempt from road tax from mid-2027. The **12 euro** STIB passes for young people and seniors are maintained. The single ticket would be indexed from **2.40** to **2.50 euros** in February 2027, according to BRUZZ.

Road safety: nearly **20%** fewer injured in the first half of 2026, according to Brussels Mobility. The number of seriously injured falls by **34%**.
