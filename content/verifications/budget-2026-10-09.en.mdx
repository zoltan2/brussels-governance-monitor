## Verification carried out on 9 October 2026

**Result: factual errors and attributions corrected.**

### Method

The sources cited by the card were opened one by one and each key figure was compared with the text of its source. About 45 items were checked.

### Corrections applied

- Budgetary effort in the government agreement: around EUR 1 billion by 2029 (RTBF and La DH), not 1.2 billion.
- Credit lines at the end of 2025: only the withdrawal by Belfius (EUR 500 million) is established; the ING line was due to expire on 31 December 2025 and the bank said it had taken no decision.
- Debt and interest: the tripling since 2016 and the EUR 399 million in interest come from IBSA (Focus no. 73), not from the Court of Audit.
- 2025 deficit: EUR 1.6 billion according to the government press release of 4 October 2026.
- Registration duties: ceiling of the allowance, not an exemption threshold.
- Administrative reform: 25 bodies grouped into four pillars, confirmed by La DH of 13 February 2026; the 20 to 30% cost reduction, found in no source, has been removed.
- Removed for lack of a source: real loss of 6% over two years, the billion in savings attributed to the February 2026 conclave, the EUR 32.8 million cost of the Be Home bonus.

### Limits

Three items could not be verified and are left unchanged: a subscriber-only Le Soir article, the 2024 deficit of EUR 1.552 billion and the sale price of the official residence. Sections dated before 1 October 2026 and BX1 sources were not reread.
