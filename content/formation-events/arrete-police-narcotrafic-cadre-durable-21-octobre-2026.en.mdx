## The fact

On **Wednesday 21 October 2026**, the Minister-President signs a police order setting a **lasting framework** for the fight against drug trafficking. Since the regional strategy was launched in 2024, the orders had been extended every six months.

Throughout the Region, the order bans fireworks and the sale of nitrous oxide, except to professionals. In the **19 sensitive zones** ("hotspots"), it bans riding and parking **electric scooters, shared or private**: between January 2025 and July 2026, a scooter was used in more than a quarter of the shootings linked to drug trafficking, to shoot or to flee. As shared scooters disappear from the whole Region on 1 January 2027, the ban will then cover only private scooters.

## What remains in force

Each zone is assessed every three months by the mayor, who may ask for zones to be added or removed. The following remain in force: the ban on going there for the sole purpose of buying drugs, the ban on drinking alcohol in public and on selling it to take away between 10 pm and 6 am (horeca excepted), and identity checks, administrative seizures and security searches, 24 hours a day. In the event of a serious threat, the mayor may restrict traffic to residents, ban gatherings, and impose the night-time closure of establishments, a curfew or a speed limit.

## What remains open

The text of the order has not been published and the arrangements for enforcement are not detailed. See the [Security](/en/domains/security) card.
