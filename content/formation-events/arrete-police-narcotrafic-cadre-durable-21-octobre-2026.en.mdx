## The fact

On **Monday 19 October 2026**, the Minister-President signs a police order setting an **integrated framework** for the fight against drug trafficking; he announces it on Wednesday 21 October, the day the text and the list of the 19 sensitive zones are published on safe.brussels. The order repeals and replaces the one of 2 September 2026; since 2024, the orders had been extended every six months.

Throughout the Region, the order bans fireworks and the sale of nitrous oxide, except to professionals. In the **19 sensitive zones** ("hotspots"), it bans riding and parking **electric scooters, shared or private**: between January 2025 and July 2026, a scooter was used in more than a quarter of the shootings linked to drug trafficking, to shoot or to flee. As shared scooters disappear from the whole Region on 1 January 2027, the ban will then cover only private scooters.

## What remains in force

Each zone is assessed at least once a quarter by the mayor, who asks for it to be kept on or removed from the list; the mayor may also ask for a zone to be added. The following remain in force: the ban on going there for the sole purpose of buying drugs, the ban on drinking alcohol in public and on selling it to take away between 10 pm and 6 am (horeca excepted), and identity checks, administrative seizures and security searches, 24 hours a day. In the event of a serious threat, the mayor may restrict traffic to residents, ban gatherings, and impose the night-time closure of establishments, a curfew or a speed limit.

## What remains open

The measures specific to the sensitive zones no longer have an end date; those that apply across the whole Region remain in force until 15 November 2027. The mayors' quarterly assessments will determine which zones stay on the list. See the [Security](/en/domains/security) card.
