## Brussels Airspace: Citizen Mobilization Against RNP 07L Route

The week was marked by a major escalation in contestation against the RNP 07L landing route. On Sunday, September 13, the citizen collective Free Air 4 Brussels organized a demonstration at Elisabeth Park in Koekelberg, drawing several hundred participants. **Up to two hundred Brussels families** announced their intent to join the lawsuit filed by **42 families** from the Flemish periphery, which will be heard on February 8, 2027, before the Dutch-speaking court. The next hearing in the regional action is scheduled for January 11, 2027. The federal government must make a final decision by October 1 at the latest.

## Employment: Improved Rate, but Public Service Tensions

Brussels recorded an employment rate of **64.7 %** in the second quarter of 2026 (up 1 percentage point), against an ILO-based unemployment rate of **12.7 %**. The regional target remains **70 % by 2030**. The Training Plan approved by the COCOF on September 10 complements this dynamic. However, the Constitutional Court validated on September 10 a time limitation on unemployment benefits — a federal reform that reshapes Brussels' social protection landscape. In Molenbeek-Saint-Jean, the OCMW counted **1,740 persons excluded from unemployment** as of August 31, with a projection of **16.3 million euros** that the municipality would bear from 2027 to 2029.

## Social Housing: Investments and Governance Tensions

The social housing sector remains under strain. According to L'Echo, multiple SISP's (public social housing companies) refuse to deposit their liquidity in the common treasury managed by the SLRB, which has accumulated a deficit of **approximately 60 million euros** since March 2026. Meanwhile, Molenbeek-Saint-Jean calculated **16.3 million euros** as the share of social integration income that would remain the municipality's responsibility from 2027 to 2029, and is studying an appeal against the removal of federal PAS funding.

## Mobility: Good Move Postponed, Accessibility Challenges

According to BRUZZ, the agenda item on Good Move evaluation and the next regional mobility plan's preparation was postponed for the **fourth time** at government on September 10, 2026. The applicable procedure remains that of the October 13, 2023 ordinance; Good Move remains in force until the next plan takes effect. On accessibility, STIB reports progress: approximately **500 stops have been adapted since 2019**, meaning **one quarter of roughly 2,200 surface stops**, while **59 of 69 metro and pre-metro stations** now have elevators. T3000 trams will arrive on lines 39 and 44 in spring 2027.

## Cleanliness and Waste Collection Reform

Waste collection reform is expected in 2027, featuring two zone types based on density. The Region plans **120 sorting spaces over the legislature**, including two pilot districts (30 spaces each) without door-to-door collection. Financing will rest on extended producer responsibility for single-use plastics — a novel model for Brussels.

## Social Affairs: Fresh Start Package and Housing Issues

The Brussels government approved on September 10 the **Fresh Start Package**, **36 measures** for intimate partner violence victims. A single entry point will be created within Olista to guide victims without requiring a prior complaint. Meanwhile, the Region operates **63 accommodation beds for persons with addiction issues**, a figure unchanged since 2023 according to BRUZZ and L'Avenir.
