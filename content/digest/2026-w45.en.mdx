## Housing: 66 social homes, the winter truce, the SLRB

**66 social homes** have been completed near Place Anneessens; the first tenants are expected in early 2027. According to BRUZZ, checks on Airbnb rentals have already brought in 5 million euros in fines. The winter truce on evictions is in force from 1 November 2026 to 15 March 2027. The SLRB's 131 million appear to be approved in the 2027 budget, with a new tranche of 120 million, according to BRUZZ.

## Employment: 102,550 jobseekers, of whom 37,564 depend on a CPAS

At the end of October 2026, Actiris counts **102,550 registered jobseekers**, 6.6% more over one year, for an unemployment rate of **16.5%**. **37,564** registrants depend on a CPAS public welfare centre, up **97.3%** over one year. According to La DH, the Region lost 1,039,456.59 euros to fraud involving paid educational leave.

## 2027 budget: 144 million in savings on staff, according to BRUZZ

According to BRUZZ, the 2027 budget provides for **144 million** euros in savings on staff compared with the end of 2025 and 55 million on consultancy. It also counts, for 2027, on 40 million euros in returns linked to employment and growth.

## Urban planning: the planning amnesty to be revised, the freeze on vacant sites on appeal

In an opinion from September, the Council of State asks for a fundamental revision of the draft ordinance establishing the single urban planning regularisation declaration (DLUU); Parliament has not taken up the matter since. On the freeze on vacant sites of more than 0.5 hectares, in force until 31 December 2026, the court of appeal will deliver its ruling by **4 January 2027** at the latest.

## Institutions: cancelled committees, home searches

According to Le Soir, **26 committee meetings** of the Brussels Parliament were cancelled in 2025-2026; the four previous years counted between 1 and 14. At federal level, the Chamber adopts on 6 November 2026 the law on home searches, against which the Brussels Parliament had voted a resolution on 2 October.

The Brussels Region returns to code green for drought on **5 November 2026**, after the yellow alert in place since 23 July. The Sustainable Building Facilitator helpdesk of Brussels Environment has been suspended since September 2026, with no date for resumption.

## Overflights: the RNP 07L remains in force

According to BRUZZ, the test phase of the RNP 07L route ended on 31 October 2026, but the route remains in force with no end date. The federal minister is requesting studies on alternating it with a slightly curved variant.
