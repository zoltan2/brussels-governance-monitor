## Budget: the Court of Audit rejects the 2025 accounts, the 2027 budget in tables

The Court of Audit issues an **adverse opinion** on the Region's 2025 general account, for the second year running; consolidated gross debt reaches **EUR 17.7 billion** at the end of 2025. The official tables of the 2027 budget confirm a deficit of **EUR 719 million**. The draft ordinance on personal income tax lowers the regional surcharges from 32.591% to 31.258%.

## Overflights: seven municipalities against the RNP 07L route

The Region let the deadline for an appeal to the Council of State expire, according to De Standaard and Le Soir. Seven municipalities have filed or are preparing an appeal, BRUZZ reports; the Region says it wants to join them, together with the COCOF and the COCOM.

## Mobility: the shared bike framework adopted in committee

On 10 November 2026 the Mobility Committee adopted the legal framework for shared mobility: **2,500 bikes** per operator until May 2028. Nearly 15,000 cyclists are waiting for a secure parking space, BRUZZ reports.

**See the [shared mobility dossier →](/en/dossiers/mobilite-partagee)**

## Social: the Brussels Deal in committee, free care reinforced

According to La DH, the Brussels health minister expects **1,000 more people on the street** if federal funding for the Brussels Deal stops, and puts the cost of homelessness at EUR 120 million a year. The Red Cross and Doctors of the World are reinforcing the Refugee Medical Point, which receives about 80 visits a day.

## Early childhood: measures at the ONE

According to RTBF, the government of the Wallonia-Brussels Federation is removing a member of the ONE and launching a forensic procedure; its commissioner ensures continuity of management. No change is announced for crèches.

## Heat: 48.7% of the territory covered by vegetation

According to Brussels Environment, vegetation covers **48.7%** of the regional territory in 2023. Help4Trees counts at least 72,450 tall trees cleared for felling by permit between 2010 and 2025.
