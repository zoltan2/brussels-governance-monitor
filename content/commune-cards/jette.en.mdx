## Avenue du Sacré-Cœur: separated cycle path dropped, subsidy to be repaid (October 2026)

According to BX1, picking up a BRUZZ report, the municipality is giving up the segregated cycle path planned on avenue du Sacré-Cœur, even though a permit allowed it. The path was to be a link in the regional cycle highway C212. To keep the parking spaces, the college opts for an ochre cycle lane painted on the carriageway, with no physical separation. The municipality had nonetheless negotiated spaces with SNCB in the car park behind Jette station. Acting mayor Thomas Naessens (Ecolo-Groen) acknowledges that « a cycle path is much safer ». The municipality must repay in full the subsidy of more than 150,000 euros from the Bike for Brussels fund, managed by the King Baudouin Foundation. The works are announced for spring 2027.

## Ring: a new study on closing exit 8 at Wemmel (October 2026)

Flanders plans to close exit 8 of the Ring at Wemmel. In return, it would add an on-ramp to the inner Ring in Jette and turn exit 9 into a full interchange. According to BRUZZ, the municipality has opposed this for years, fearing more traffic and air pollution. The Region does not support the closure without solutions to limit the shift of traffic. The details are on the [Mobility](/en/domains/mobility) card.

## A transparency score to improve

Jette has 54,000 inhabitants and is led by Claire Vandevivere (Les Engagés) with a renewed Engagés-MR-Ecolo coalition. The municipality has the lowest transparency score among the 19 communes.

## Transparency: 2 out of 6 criteria

The **budget** is published online and the **mandate registry** is accessible via the transparency page (attendance, remunerations). However, council **minutes** are only partially available, the **livestream** is being set up following a citizen petition (300+ signatures), and there is no **Open Data** portal or confirmed **participation platform**.

## The livestream project

A citizen petition with over 300 signatures pushed the municipality to announce that council sessions would be livestreamed. Deployment is underway.

## Recent developments

According to BX1 (20 February 2026), Jette's municipal council decided to send the Region a **formal notice** requesting the reopening of **Avenue Broustin**, a key structural road. The **green parking zone** has been extended to new neighbourhoods since February 2026.

## Jette intervenes in two proceedings against RNP 07L (26 August 2026)

On the evening of **Wednesday 26 August 2026**, the municipality announced in a statement that it is **intervening in two ongoing proceedings** against the **RNP 07L** flight path: the **environmental cessation action** brought in 2018 by the Brussels Region, and the **challenge to Brussels Airport's environmental permit**.

This executes the **motion adopted on 4 March 2026** by the municipal council, carried by the Ecolo-Groen, LBJette and MR-VLD majority, which already committed the commune to two legal actions. Jette thereby joins Schaerbeek, Evere, Berchem-Sainte-Agathe and, on the Flemish side, Lennik.

**It is the substance of its position that sets Jette apart.** The municipality writes that "simply scrapping RNP07 cannot on its own constitute a solution", since returning to the VOR07 path would displace the nuisance rather than remove it. It calls instead for a **cap limiting landings over the north-western municipalities**, together with noise-level and timing restrictions.

The full file, including the Envisa study and the municipal legal front, is documented in the dossier on flights over Brussels.
