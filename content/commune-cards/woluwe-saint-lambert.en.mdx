## Brussels' participation pioneer

Woluwe-Saint-Lambert has approximately 61,000 inhabitants. In 2008, it became the **first Brussels municipality** to adopt a citizen participation code. Olivier Maingain (DéFI) leads the municipality with Les Engagés.

## Transparency: 5 out of 6 criteria

The **budget** and council **minutes** (dedicated page organised by year) are published online, the council has been **livestreamed** since 2019, the **mandate registry** is accessible, and the municipality runs a **participatory budget** of EUR 200,000/year with 60 to 80 annual meetings. No dedicated **Open Data** portal.

## Citizen participation: a model

Woluwe-Saint-Lambert devotes **EUR 200,000 per year** to its participatory budget, the highest among Brussels municipalities. The 2008 participation code structures 60 to 80 citizen meetings per year.

## Recent developments

A rare achievement: Woluwe-Saint-Lambert is the **only Brussels municipality to lower its property tax** (−15 points), thanks to a **budget surplus**. The **Poseidon pool** is closed for 6 months of renovation works.

## M7 metro: provisional court win against nuisances (7 July 2026)

The municipality and 28 residents obtained, on **7 July 2026** before the justice of the peace, a provisional agreement limiting the circulation of faulty M7 metro trains (vibrations and noise) on line 1, along Avenue de Broqueville and Avenue Vandervelde: these trains will no longer run between 10pm and 6am on weekdays, nor from Friday 10pm to Monday 6am, until **30 September 2026**. The proceedings, launched in May 2024, still await a ruling on the merits, expected in the second quarter of 2027. The alderman for Mobility, Grégory Matgen, called the agreement "a first relief", stressing it is "not the end of our fight". Context: the M7 trains have suffered a wheel defect since 2021, with a repair programme covering around 1,000 wheels and replacement planned from October 2025 to September 2026.

Sources: Municipality of Woluwe-Saint-Lambert, official statement (7 July 2026); RTBF (7 July 2026); L'Avenir, La Libre (2 July 2026).


## Removal of the Vergote tunnel announced (25 July 2026)

The Brussels mobility minister announced the **removal of the Vergote tunnel**, located under **boulevard Brand Whitlock**, on the boundary between the commune and **Schaerbeek**.

The removal is part of the **redevelopment of boulevard Reyers**, where **pedestrian crossings will be brought back to street level**. The decision was welcomed by the commune's mayor.

Neither the decree nor the works timetable has been published at this stage. The regional detail of this measure appears on the `mobility` domain card.

## Boulevard de la Woluwe: permit application to make the cycle path permanent (public inquiry until 1 October 2026)

Brussels Mobility is applying for a **planning permit** (reference **18/PFD/2030166**) to regularise the trial phase and permanently lay out the **two-way cycle path** on **boulevard de la Woluwe**, in Woluwe-Saint-Lambert, together with the redesign of junctions. The public inquiry runs from **Wednesday 2 September** to **Thursday 1 October 2026**; the consultation committee meets on **Friday 16 October 2026**.

According to BRUZZ, the project restores **ten parking spaces**, keeps **47%** of the original road capacity (compared with 40% during the trial), fells **two trees** and plants **seventeen**.

Sources: [openpermits.brussels, permit 18/PFD/2030166 (accessed 28 September 2026)](https://openpermits.brussels/fr/_18/PFD/2030166) ; [BRUZZ (28 September 2026)](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/mobiliteit/brussel-wil-fietspad-langs-de-woluwelaan-permanent-maken-2026-09-28). Confidence: official for the permit application and the dates of the public inquiry and the consultation committee (openpermits.brussels); unconfirmed for the project figures, given by BRUZZ alone.

## M7 metro: further wheel replacements and vibration measurements (20 November 2026)

According to BruxellesToday of 20 November 2026, which cites BX1, STIB replaced the 152 defective wheels of the M7 trainsets on 30 September 2026 and will replace more than 1,292 other wheels of the first series from 2027, with the manufacturer CAF. A measuring device is installed at Maelbeek station; the measurements, checked weekly, were passed to the expert appointed by the justice of the peace of Woluwe-Saint-Lambert. The provisional agreement of 7 July 2026 ran until 30 September 2026; the article read does not say whether it was extended. Details are on the [Mobility](/en/domains/mobility) page.

Source: [BruxellesToday (20 November 2026)](https://www.bruxellestoday.be/mobilite/nuisances-sonores-vibrations-stib-metro-remplacement.html). Confidence: *unconfirmed* (a single newsroom, which cites BX1, not consulted; no STIB or municipal document was read).

## Freezing weather: a ten-place municipal emergency shelter and outreach rounds (27 November 2026)

According to a BX1 report of Friday 27 November 2026, the municipality of Woluwe-Saint-Lambert activates its "extreme cold" plan when temperatures drop below zero: it opens an emergency shelter where up to ten homeless people can spend the night, take a shower and have a hot meal, and its street educators carry out outreach rounds. The arrangement is part of the municipality's cold-weather plan, which runs from mid-November to mid-March. According to the municipality's prevention officer, quoted by BX1, it relies on a small team working very heavy hours. The winter places announced at regional level by the Common Community Commission are on the [Social](/en/domains/social) card.

Source: [BX1 (27 November 2026)](https://bx1.be/categories/news/la-commune-de-woluwe-saint-lambert-met-en-place-son-plan-froid-extreme-cela-demande-une-debauche-denergie-enorme/). Confidence: *unconfirmed* (a single outlet; no municipal page dated November 2026 consulted).
