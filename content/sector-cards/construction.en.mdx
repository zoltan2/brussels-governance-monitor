## Regional 2026 budget voted: framework for the sector

On **27 March 2026**, the Brussels Parliament voted the 2026 regional budget (plenary 53 in favour / 32 against). Three structuring envelopes for construction:

- **SLRB/BGHM: €400M** injected over the legislature (February 2026 coalition agreement, distinct from the €61M PSRD 2026-2035 tranche announced in July) + **1,000 public housing units** announced
- **Roads: +€10M** additional in 2026 (AI maintenance, night works)
- **Station security: €28M** allocated for Brussels-South cleanliness + security (on top of €10M RPD Midi/Nord)

The **bMa Strategic Plan** (Bouwmeester / chief architect Lisa De Visscher), approved on 16 April 2026, provides for **bMa opinion delivered in half the time** for projects ≥ 5,000 sqm — first operational measure of the RPD goal of cutting permit timelines by 50%.

Sources: 2026 budget voted in plenary (BX1/BRUZZ 27 March 2026); BRUZZ, "bMa Strategic Plan approved" (16 April 2026).

## Infrabel: €57.8M for 7 accessible Brussels stations (24 April 2026)

The federal Council of Ministers approved on Friday 24 April 2026, on the proposal of Mobility Minister Jean-Luc Crucke (Les Engagés), an amendment to Infrabel's 2023-2032 multi-year investment plan. **€57.8 million** is released to raise platforms and improve access at **7 Brussels stations**: Brussels-South (Midi), Etterbeek, Schaerbeek, Haren-South, Uccle-Stalle, Forest-East, Bordet. Funding from the postponement of two major rail projects (Rail Ghent-Terneuzen, accessibility of other stations) which will not start before 2030.

Sources: [BRUZZ](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/mobiliteit/van-etterbeek-tot-vorst-oost-zeven-brusselse-stations-worden-toegankelijker-2026-04-24), [BX1](https://bx1.be/categories/mobilite/infrabel-lance-de-nouveaux-projets-ferroviaires-57-millions-deuros-prevus-pour-les-gares-bruxelloises/) (24 April 2026).

## Metro 3 under the North Station: officially compromised (23 April 2026)

The 18th hearing of the Brussels Parliament's Special Metro 3 Commission (23 April 2026) heard **Henri Ernst**, CEO of Galère SRL (SM Progrès consortium appointed by Beliris): "We assume there is little chance the project [under the North Station] will proceed."

Beliris recently asked the consortium to complete only the surface urban redesign **around** the station (rue du Progrès, rue d'Aerschot) — no longer the underground cavity. A new **V.E.R.T. technique** would require **+€75.119M** on top of the €22.56M already spent, **with no feasibility guarantee**. The site has been idle for several years.

See the full dossier: [Metro 3](/en/dossiers/metro-3).

Sources: [BRUZZ Urbanism](https://www.bruzz.be/actua/stedenbouw/topman-bouwfirma-kans-klein-dat-metro-3-project-onder-noordstation-er-komt-2026-04-23), [La DH Bruxelles](https://www.dhnet.be/regions/bruxelles/bruxelles-mobilite/2026/04/23/le-chantier-du-metro-3-sous-la-gare-du-nord-a-larret-pour-plusieurs-annees-KVXPKWRUEZAO5GXOX6OIHNQIQI/) (23 April 2026).

## Housing Fund: mortgage lending resumed on 13 July 2026

Mortgage loans from the **Brussels-Capital Region Housing Fund** had been suspended since **1 July 2025** for lack of financing: banks refused to lend to the body in the absence of a regional government and a voted budget. A resumption on 1 July 2026 had been announced on 23 April 2026 in a Brussels Parliament committee. During the suspension, the Fund placed part of its staff on temporary lay-off from 1 March 2026.

The Fund announced the resumption of its mortgage lending on **13 July 2026**, after the regional guarantee was granted and its call for financing succeeded. Rates range from **3.75% to 5%**. The Region set the guaranteed borrowing ceiling for 2026 at **100 million euros**. **Ecoreno** loans had been available again since 1 January 2026.

Sources: [Housing Fund (in French)](https://fonds.brussels/fr/a-propos/nouvelles-fonds-du-logement/reprise-de-lactivite-des-credits-hypothecaires-au-fonds-du) (13 July 2026); [Brussels-Capital Region, press release on the guarantee (in French)](https://be.brussels/fr/propos-de-la-region/structure-et-organisation/parlement-ministres/le-gouvernement-regional/karine-lalieux/actualites/le-gouvernement-bruxellois-octroie-la-garantie-pour-la-reprise-des-credits-hypothecaires-du-fonds-du); [La DH Bruxelles](https://www.dhnet.be/regions/bruxelles/2026/04/23/suspendus-depuis-lete-2025-les-credits-hypothecaires-du-fonds-du-logement-doivent-reprendre-en-juillet-Z7VJSNXN7NHGDAXO6F5OQSQBIQ/) (23 April 2026). Confidence: *official*.

## The brownfield dilemma

The 18-month moratorium announced in the agreement covers **9 sites** (Keyenbempt, Calevoet, Josaphat, Meylemeersch, etc.) and represents **~800 potential housing units per year**, nearly a quarter of Brussels' annual housing production. Wiels, Avijl and Donderberg are preserved. Chant des Cailles and Dames Blanches are confirmed as construction sites.

Source: [Moustique](https://www.moustique.be/notre-epoque/les-infos/2026/02/09/crise-du-logement-si-la-region-bruxelloise-interdit-la-construction-sur-neuf-friches-est-ce-la-fin-des-logements-abordables-dans-la-capitale-ZIPNTNWJFNE3NLPOXCM76VUBCM/) (February 2026).

## Sector context: worst crisis in a decade

- **−1,402 construction firms** in the Brussels-Capital Region in 2024 — first decline in ten years
- **Residential construction** down 7.3% in 2024 nationally
- **No recovery expected before 2026-2027** at the earliest (Embuild)
- **Neo (Heysel)** officially restarted in the 12 February 2026 agreement

Sources: [Embuild, market outlook](https://embuild.be/fr/la-construction-toujours-en-difficult%C3%A9-pas-de-reprise-pr%C3%A9vue-avant-2026-voire-2027); La Libre, "the greatest challenge since World War II" (February 2026).
