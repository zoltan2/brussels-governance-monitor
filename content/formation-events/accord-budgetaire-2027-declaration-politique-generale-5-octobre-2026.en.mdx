## The fact

On the evening of **Sunday 4 October 2026**, after a two-day conclave, the Brussels government reaches an agreement on the 2027 budget: the deficit is to be brought down to **719 million euros**, from 957 million in 2026, with a return to balance in 2029. On **Monday 5 October 2026**, the Minister-President delivers his general policy statement before the Brussels Parliament and the United Assembly. The discussion takes place on Tuesday 6 October 2026.

## What remains open

The budget tables were not given to members of parliament before the sitting; the Minister-President undertook to send the multi-year table before the debate, according to L'Avenir. On the 131 million euros intended for the SLRB, the Socialist ministers and the Budget minister say the funds are confirmed, while the Minister-President states that the matter was not dealt with. The measures are followed in the [Budget](/en/domains/budget) card.
