## Budget: the 2027 budget documents are at Parliament

The Brussels government has sent the 2027 budget documents to Parliament, the Budget Minister confirmed on Friday 30 October 2026. The deficit target remains **719 million euros**, for an effort of **563 million**. The plenary vote is being considered for 18 December 2026. On online gaming, according to BRUZZ, Wallonia stays at 11%, with Brussels not raising its rate.

## Overflights: Brussels calls the Flemish conflict of interest unfounded

In a note dated Thursday 29 October 2026, the Brussels government deems unfounded the conflict of interest invoked by Flanders against federal measures on Brussels Airport. It wants to put the matter on the agenda of the Concertation Committee of **16 November 2026**.

## Housing: socialised rents, Be Home and social rental agencies

According to La DH, the 2027 budget provides for **3.2 million euros** in savings on rent socialisation. Municipalities calculate an average increase of 180 to 200 euros for the households concerned. According to La Libre, 5 million euros are planned in 2027 to open the Be Home premium to landlords below the reference rent, for 164 or 328 euros per year. According to notaries, **43.6%** of Brussels buyers purchase outside the Region in 2025.

## Social: 130 convalescence beds, seven alternatives to nursing homes

Iriscare is relaunching its call for **130 convalescence beds** and raising its envelope from 250,000 to 580,000 euros; applications are open until 30 November 2026. Iriscare is also funding seven projects offering alternatives to nursing homes.

## Security: the FIPA operation of 29 October

On 29 October 2026, an operation coordinated by the federal police mobilises more than **400 personnel**: 16 judicial and 20 administrative arrests. According to BRUZZ, the military operations Urban Shield and Green Shield cost about 4 million euros net up to the end of September.

## Employment: a proposed premium, employment areas

According to La Libre, a draft ordinance from the opposition seeks an employment premium of up to 600 euros per year; the government has decided nothing on this point. Statbel publishes experimental employment areas, with Saint-Josse and the City of Brussels at the top of the Region.

## Mobility: the driving licence, the Palais du Midi, road aggression

From 1 January 2027, Flanders requires the theory and practical parts of the driving licence to be taken in the same Region; Brussels has two test centres. The Council of State rejects three requests for suspension against the partial dismantling of the Palais du Midi. According to a Vias survey, **45%** of Brussels respondents admit to aggressive behaviour on the road.
