## Taxis: acceleration of zero-emission timeline

The Brussels government confirmed on 1 October 2026 the obligation for all newly registered taxis to be zero-emission by 1 January 2027. Two additional years of operation are granted for thermal vehicles in circulation, extending the maximum age from 7 to 9 years for thermal cars and from 8 to 10 years for electric vehicles. The region aims for between **100 and 150 fast-charging stations** by end of 2028. Approximately **3,129 taxis** operate in Brussels, of which **440 are electric**.

## Regional budget 2027: 238 million euros to find

The budget conclave opens on 3 October 2026. The government must identify approximately **238 million euros** in adjustments to reduce the deficit to around 719 million in 2027. Negotiations with the federal level on Beliris (**50 million euros**) and the Brussels Deal (**43 million euros**) remain blocked. The SLRB is still waiting for its **131 million euros**. The government aims to reach an agreement before its policy statement on 5 October.

## Plan Gares approved: **3 million** for 2026

The Brussels government approved on 1 October the first phase of Plan Gares, allocated **3 million euros** for 2026. This funding finances the cleaning of North and South stations, replacement of obsolete cameras, lighting improvements at the covered street at South Station, and strengthening of social patrols. According to BRUZZ, the plan includes **5 million euros** in 2027, then **10 million per year** for the last two years of the legislature. This measure is part of the regional Security Plan and complements cleanliness commitments.

## Property tax: flat-rate system for children and doubled Be Home premium

The draft ordinance approved on first reading will convert the property tax reduction for children into a flat-rate system from 2027 onwards. According to a study by the Family League of 1 October, this measure would save **9 million euros annually**. In exchange, the Be Home premium will double: from **164 to 328 euros per year**. The study estimates that every family with a property tax exceeding **1,250 euros** would lose out under this flat-rate regime.

## Flanders reduces cultural subsidies to Brussels

The Flemish Community is reducing by **30 %** its cultural subsidies to Brussels municipalities starting 2027. The budget drops from **3.7 to 2.6 million euros** for Dutch-language cultural policy and Dutch-language libraries. Bozar loses **760,000 euros** in Flemish subsidy from 2027, approximately **2 %** of its budget. Its director notes that the Brussels region subsidy amounts to **150,000 euros per year**. The Screen Flanders fund, which co-financed films and series in Brussels, is abolished as of 1 January 2027.

## Molenbeek-Saint-Jean: regional loan request in November

The Brussels minister for local authorities indicated to Parliament on 2 October that Molenbeek is preparing a request for regional intervention. The government decision is expected in November 2026. The municipality posted a **11.6 million euro** deficit in 2025 and a cumulative deficit of **24.4 million euros**. According to the minister, the federal unemployment reform increases the social assistance budget by approximately **3.5 million euros in 2027**.

[See Molenbeek-Saint-Jean →](/en/municipalities/molenbeek-saint-jean)

## Brussels Parliament: resolution against home searches

The Brussels Parliament adopted on 2 October 2026, in plenary session, a resolution against the federal bill authorizing home searches for persons without legal residence status. Two parties from the regional majority voted with the opposition, while a third party voted against. The same day, a request to examine urgent sanctions for elected officials breaching separation of powers was rejected.

## City of Brussels: eight properties offered for sale

The City of Brussels is offering eight buildings and houses for public sale on Biddit, with base prices ranging from **300,000 to 3.5 million euros**. According to BRUZZ, the renovation project of the former National Bank printing house on Boulevard de Berlaimont began on 29 September 2026. This operation is part of municipal property management.

## Assizes of Brussels Elders: housing and home care

The first Assizes of the Elders of the City of Brussels brought together thirteen municipalities and addressed four themes. According to the report, raised demands include maintaining information on paper and physical counters, improving sidewalk accessibility, developing adapted housing, and strengthening home care.
