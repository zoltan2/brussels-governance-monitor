summary: "On Saturday 25 July 2026, on the fifth day of a full strike begun on 21 July, the Brussels government and the firefighters' joint union front reached a pre-agreement. It covers new recruitment campaigns from 2026 with broader reinforcement in 2027, rapid integration of recruits into a waiting regime, the creation of recruitment reserves, the allocation of the 10 million euro federal envelope (over 8 million for operational staff and the 112 emergency call centre, 2 million for infrastructure) and the maintenance of pay scales and statutory conditions. The unions called for the action to be suspended, not ended: the rank and file must still validate the agreement."
impact: "This resolution ends the longest mobilisation by the Brussels fire service documented by BGM, in a service whose operational availability had fallen to 17% of staff on 25 July. It breaks the deadlock opened in May 2026 by the shift reorganisation plan and lifts the government agreement's SIAMU staff reinforcement commitment out of 'delayed' status. The agreement nonetheless remains conditional: it is a pre-agreement subject to validation by the union rank and file, and a rejection would restart the dispute."
