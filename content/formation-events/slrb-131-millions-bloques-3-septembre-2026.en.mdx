## The fact

On **Thursday 3 September 2026**, the MR chair told *La Libre*, then confirmed to *BRUZZ*, that his party was blocking the release of regional funding to the **SLRB**: the **2026 allocation of 131 million euros** will not be paid for as long as **Lotfi Mostefa** chairs the **Foyer anderlechtois**, to which he was re-elected on 17 August 2026.

## What makes the fact verifiable

The **housing state secretary's cabinet confirms that the 131 million expected during the summer has not arrived**. This is therefore not an announced intention but a payment recorded as absent by its intended recipient.

## The consequences described

In the short term the parent body, which supervises sixteen public service property companies, could fail to meet its invoices from September 2026. In the medium term it would have to sell further buildings, while summary proceedings have since 13 July 2026 contested the sale of two sites meant to raise about 52 million for the debt reduction plan, and 62,234 households sit on the social housing waiting list.

## The positions

The Brussels PS speaks of bluster and a heavy political attack. The PTB accuses the liberals of holding social housing tenants hostage. The MR says it wants to put the minister for local authorities before his responsibilities.
