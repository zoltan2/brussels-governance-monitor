## Shootings and Security: Double Counting Reveals Crisis Scale

The Brussels Public Prosecutor's Office quantified the wave of shootings on 28 August: sixty-nine since 1 January 2026, one hundred two in 2025, approximately one hundred fifty-two explosions since February 2025. Meanwhile, the federal police count fifty-seven shootings over the first seven months of 2026. These two counts, which do not overlap and whose respective scopes remain unclear, now coexist on the [security page](/en/domains/security).

The prosecutor's office framework includes forty-nine magistrates out of sixty-three authorized positions. The prosecutor estimates that ten million euros in additional resources are needed. On 26 August, five hundred fifty officers — including seventy military personnel — conducted a coordinated control operation at Clemenceau, Yser, Rogier and Hankar.

Meanwhile, the [Regional Security Council](/en/domains/security) on 26 August approved forty federal police officers and twenty mobile cameras within twenty days. A unified command under the chief of the Brussels-Capital/Ixelles police zone will coordinate the six police zones. The federal Interior Minister separately announced a reserve platoon. These three announcements are not cumulative, and no source consolidates an actual operational total.

The selection for regional commissioner of security and drugs lists eleven candidates — an increase from the eight originally announced.

## Mobility and Infrastructure: Tunnels and E-Scooters

The federal Beliris fund allocates one hundred million euros to renovate the Belliard and Law tunnels, according to L'Echo. Work was supposed to begin in 2024 and has been postponed twice. No schedule has been published.

As of 1 September 2026, helmets become mandatory for electric scooters with construction speeds exceeding twenty kilometres per hour.

## Municipal Budget and Poverty: Brussels City Raises Taxes, One Municipality in Distress

City of Brussels has raised its additional property tax percentages from **2,950 to 3,457**, a **17.2 %** increase, with notices mailed from 17 August 2026. The increase is fully offset for owner-occupants through a flat municipal deduction of one hundred euros and the regional Be-Home premium. The doubling of this premium — one hundred sixty-four euros in 2026, three hundred twenty-eight euros in 2027 — is a regional commitment that absorbs the municipal increase rather than relieving taxpayers.

## Brussels Airspace: 3,815 Noise Limit Breaches in Four Months

Brussels Environment recorded **3,815 exceedances** of Brussels noise standards between January and April 2026. Averaged monthly, the rate doubles: four hundred seventy-six per month in 2025, nine hundred fifty-four per month in 2026. Approximately **80 %** of breaches originate from a single monitoring station in Molenbeek-Saint-Jean, making the finding heavily dependent on one measurement point.

## Saint-Josse-ten-Noode: Legal Action Against Region

Saint-Josse-ten-Noode announces judicial and administrative recourse against the Region, alleging deliberate financial asphyxiation. The municipality learned on 30 July of refusal to approve its 2024 accounts — for the second time. Without approved accounts, there is no 2026 budget; without a budget, no full access to regional funding. It is excluded from the one hundred fifty million euro package for municipalities in difficulty.
