Climate · Region · 01 / 06

Less than half of Brussels is covered by vegetation.

In 2023, vegetation covers 48.7% of the regional territory. Excluding the Sonian Forest and the Bois de la Cambre, 42.6%.

According to Brussels Environment, vegetation covers 48.7% of the Region's territory in 2023. The rate adds up trees, shrubs, low vegetation and green roofs. Excluding the Sonian Forest and the Bois de la Cambre, it falls to 42.6%. The Region is also about 53% sealed.

The nuance that matters

The comparison with 2016 is imprecise

The administration had estimated coverage at 54.9% in 2016, but it considers that figure overestimated and the imagery of the two years different. A constant method has only existed since 2020: the gap between 2016 and 2023 therefore does not measure an exact loss. The Heat dossier also follows the Canopy plan and the tree fellings authorised by permit.

02

Budget · Region · 02 / 06

The Court of Audit rejects the Region's accounts, for the second year.

Adverse opinion on the 2025 general account. Consolidated gross debt reaches EUR 17.7 billion.

On Monday 9 November 2026 the Court of Audit presented its 31st Book of observations to the Brussels Parliament. It issues an adverse opinion on the 2025 general account of the regional entity, which groups the government services and 19 bodies. It is the second adverse opinion in a row. Consolidated gross debt reaches EUR 17.7 billion on 31 December 2025.

Official figure

17.7

billion euros of consolidated gross debt at the end of 2025

  • EUR 15.65 billion at the end of 2024
  • Accounts of the government services and of the SLRB rejected

The nuance that matters

An opinion on the reliability of the accounts, not on policy

An adverse opinion means the Court considers the anomalies significant and widespread in the account. It does not rule on budget choices. The tables of the 2027 budget, sent to Parliament on 30 October 2026, confirm a targeted deficit of EUR 719 million.

03

Overflights · Region and municipalities · 03 / 06

RNP 07L flight route: seven municipalities go to the Council of State.

The Region let its own appeal deadline expire, according to the press. It says it wants to join the municipalities.

Seven municipalities have filed or are preparing an annulment appeal against keeping the RNP 07L route permanently: Koekelberg, Schaerbeek, Molenbeek-Saint-Jean, the City of Brussels, Evere, Saint-Gilles and Anderlecht. On Friday 13 November 2026, in plenary session, the regional mobility minister acknowledged an error by her office in passing on the federal letter of 17 July.

Sources and details are on the card on the site.

Estimate

7

municipalities engaged in an appeal against the RNP 07L route

  • The Region's appeal deadline: sixty days after a federal letter of 17 July 2026
  • Applications announced for the COCOF and the COCOM

The nuance that matters

The Region's own appeal may be declared inadmissible

The Region is also attempting an appeal in its own name, arguing that the letter of 17 July was not a notification. The state secretary for the environment acknowledges the risk of inadmissibility. According to the press, the municipalities' appeal deadline expires on 15 or 16 November 2026; no document settles which of the two dates applies. No Council of State document or parliamentary report was consulted for this issue.

04

Mobility · Region · 04 / 06

Shared bikes: 2,500 per operator until May 2028.

The Mobility Committee of the Brussels Parliament adopted the legal framework on 10 November 2026.

On Tuesday 10 November 2026, the Mobility Committee of the Brussels Parliament adopted the draft ordinance on shared mobility tabled by the majority. Until 1 May 2028, each operator that meets the conditions may offer up to 2,500 shared bikes. The text also sets the fees and the fines.

Read the dossier on governance.brussels

Estimate

2,500

shared bikes at most per operator until 1 May 2028

  • Shared scooters banned from 1 January 2027
  • Shared mopeds kept, with one or two licences

The nuance that matters

The regime after May 2028 is not confirmed

According to the press, the final regime would provide for two or three licences of 4,000 to 8,000 bikes each. This point comes from a single newsroom, and the text still has to be voted in plenary session. The opposition considers it legally too vague.

05

Social · Federal and Region · 05 / 06

Brussels Deal: 1,000 more people on the street, according to the minister.

The federal government intends to end funding for the scheme by the end of 2026, according to the press.

In the health committee of the Brussels Parliament, the Brussels minister in charge of health said that 1,000 more people would end up on the street if 1,000 Brussels Deal places are no longer funded, because those beds are occupied. He puts the cost of handling homelessness in Brussels at EUR 120 million a year.

The minister's figures, a federal decision

These amounts are those given by the minister in committee, not figures read in a budget document. They are reported by a single outlet, and the committee report had not been published on 15 November 2026. Funding for the Brussels Deal is a federal matter.

06

Early childhood · Wallonia-Brussels Federation · 06 / 06

ONE: a member removed, a forensic procedure launched.

The government of the Wallonia-Brussels Federation takes three measures at the Office de la naissance et de l'enfance.

Following information about "potentially serious" facts, the government of the Wallonia-Brussels Federation has relieved a member of the ONE of duty, tasked its commissioner with temporarily ensuring continuity of management and launched a forensic procedure. The elements concern governance, management and the control of certain services.

Estimate

3

measures taken at the ONE by the Wallonia-Brussels Federation

  • A member relieved of duty, suspension procedure launched
  • The government commissioner ensures continuity of management

The nuance that matters

No change announced for crèches

The ONE approves, inspects and subsidises French-speaking crèches in Brussels. No change is announced for crèches or for families. The press mentions suspicions of financial misconduct and a referral to the public prosecutor, which the minister's statement does not mention in the excerpts read.
