## Two committees, two levels, one day

On **Wednesday 2 September 2026**, the Chamber's **Justice** and **Interior** committees met **jointly** in the morning on violence linked to drug trafficking in Brussels. In the afternoon, at 1.30 pm, the **Internal Affairs committee of the Brussels Parliament** heard the minister-president on the same subject.

## What the federal level announces

Twenty-one additional special units deployed in Brussels, two hundred vehicles for the federal judicial police with Brussels as a priority, 1.2 million euros a year for training, eighteen extra staff for the Brussels prosecutor's office, mobile phone jammers in the high-risk detainee wing of Haren prison, and a draft text allowing the seizure of trafficking assets without waiting for a conviction. Registration of electric scooters and a reinforced military presence remain under study.

## What the Region says

The minister-president rejected the reading that ties the wave of shootings to his government. Questioned on the reduction of subsidies to prevention associations, he challenged the effectiveness of the subsidies paid in recent years and refused to establish a direct link with the recent shootings. The PTB and Ecolo groups defend the opposite reading. No source documents to date an established link, in either direction.
