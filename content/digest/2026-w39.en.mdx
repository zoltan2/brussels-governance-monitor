## Second Warning to Saint-Josse-ten-Noode

The Brussels Government re-examined the situation of Saint-Josse-ten-Noode under the coercive guardianship procedure on 24 September 2026, and the Minister for Local Authorities announced a second warning to the municipality, after the one of 5 June 2026. Unapproved 2024 accounts, absence of a permanent financial officer since 2021, and failure to submit the 2026 budget: the municipality will have at most three months to regularise its situation. This is the last step before commissioners may be sent in, in the first coercive guardianship of a Brussels municipality since 1976.

## New SIAMU Headquarters at Tour & Taxis

The Brussels Government awarded a contract on 24 September 2026 for the construction of the new SIAMU headquarters near Tour & Taxis. This building will house the fire and emergency medical service, safe.brussels, and the regional training campus for security professionals. Construction is scheduled to begin at the end of 2029.

## Employment and Social Assistance : Balance of Unemployment Exclusions

According to Actiris monitoring published on 25 September 2026, **47.6 %** of the 15,500 Brussels residents who reached the end of their unemployment benefits in the first quarter of 2026 became beneficiaries of social assistance within three months, while **12.6 %** accessed employment, of any duration. ## Key Dossiers : Administrative Reform and URZ

In his final proposal to the unions, the Minister for Civil Service drops plans to cap pay-scale increases at 29 years of seniority, but maintains the removal of two days of leave and annual performance evaluations. He announced the end of the consultation on 24 September 2026 ; unions remain divided.

The Urban Revitalisation Zone (URZ) now covers **208 sectors** and **40 %** of Brussels residents under a Brussels Government order of 23 July 2026, effective from 1 September. This new perimeter replaces the 2020 one ; the URZ is where the Region runs its sustainable neighbourhood contracts and urban renewal contracts.
