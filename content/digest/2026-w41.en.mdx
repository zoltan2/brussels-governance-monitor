## Budget Agreement 2027 and General Policy Statement

The Brussels government concluded a budget agreement on 4 October 2026, targeting a deficit of **719 million euros** for 2027. The general policy statement was delivered on 5 October 2026 before the Brussels Parliament. A gap of **129 million euros** had to be closed to reach this target. The return to budget equilibrium remains set for 2029.

However, major disagreements persist within the coalition over the release of **131 million euros** intended for the SLRB (Brussels Social Housing Company). Socialist ministers and the Budget minister claim these credits have been confirmed, while the Minister-President asserts on 5 October that the matter was not treated by the 8 October Council of Ministers, which was interrupted on this issue. Socialist ministers left the meeting without agreement.

## Rising Unemployment, Accelerating Exclusions from Social System

**100,198** job seekers were registered with Actiris at the end of September 2026, an increase of **5.8 %** year-on-year, with an unemployment rate of **16.1 %**. Paid educational leave is hit by a new controversy: **2.3 million euros** in subsidies are frozen or claimed back for fraud, according to La DH. The Employment minister announced this to parliament on 6 October 2026. The Region had already been alerted as early as July 2023 according to BruxellesToday.

Employment policies: an « effort » of **55 million euros** in 2027 is announced, with Activa replaced by new support. The target of **70 %** employment rate by 2030 is maintained.

## 9 October Demonstration: Public Order Disruption and Disturbances

A national demonstration on 9 October 2026 in Brussels resulted in approximately **110 arrests**, according to authorities, with **21 individuals** handed over to prosecutors, including **17 minors**. The provisional police report is accompanied by eyewitness accounts of police violence reported by BX1.

Following the demonstration, STIB reports **three trams and four buses** damaged according to BRUZZ. Brussels Cleanliness (Bruxelles-Propreté) notes that up to **98 %** of white refuse sacks were not collected on 9 October in several municipalities, with four recycling centres remaining closed. Replacement collections are scheduled from 12 October 2026 onwards.

Alongside the march, prosecutors indicate they sought **eight placements** of minors, but due to lack of institutional capacity, they were released. **283 young people** from French-speaking areas await placement in secure facilities.

## Energy Renovation: Shift to Zero-Interest Loans

Renolution renovation grants will be replaced by zero-interest loans in spring 2027. The budget agreement of 4 October 2026 allocates **66 million euros** annually to this new mechanism. This reform occurs in a context where air quality remains concerning: all Brussels schools exceed WHO recommendations for nitrogen dioxide, according to modelling released on 8 October 2026 by Les Chercheurs d'air.

## Mobility and Transport: Investment and Budget Pressures

Two major tunnels are central to budget discussions: the Bailly and Vleurgat tunnels are announced for identical renovation, with an investment plan expected in November 2026. The Region also announces a new Regional Mobility Plan by end of 2028.

For 2027, measures include exemption from circulation tax for small city cars and zero-emission vehicles from mid-2027. STIB subscriptions at **12 euros** for youth and seniors are maintained. The single ticket would be indexed from **2.40** to **2.50 euros** in February 2027 according to BRUZZ.

Road safety: nearly **20 %** fewer injured in the first half of 2026, with serious injuries declining by **34 %**.
