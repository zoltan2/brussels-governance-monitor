The Low Emission Zone (LEZ) has been in force in Brussels since 2018. It progressively bans the most polluting vehicles based on their Euro emission standard. Non-compliant vehicles receive a fine when an infraction is detected by ANPR cameras.

The government agreement of February 2026 maintains the LEZ but provides for an **annual pass** (EUR 350, social rate EUR 200) as a legal alternative to per-infraction fines. The regional policy declaration also provides for a monthly fine of EUR 80; in September 2026, the fine in force remains EUR 350, at most once every three months.
