## Oxygen Brussels: **22 Measures** for SMEs in Brussels

The Brussels Government adopted the **Oxygen Brussels plan on 15 October 2026**, a comprehensive package of **22 measures** aimed at supporting small and medium-sized enterprises. Key initiatives include a new hub.info service featuring an artificial intelligence agent and a single window for access to commercial premises. This plan, championed by the Minister for Economy, represents a direct response to challenges faced by Brussels SMEs. However, no budget allocation has been announced at this stage. ## Security: Two Serious Incidents in Early October

The week was marked by two major security incidents. On **12 October**, approximately ten arrests were made at Bruxelles-Midi railway station following clashes involving nine minors. According to BX1, police used the nasse (cordon) tactic, ruled illegal in **2025**. A **41-year-old police officer** from the Brussels-Capital/Ixelles zone was fatally shot in Jette on the night of **12-13 October** while approaching a stolen vehicle. The Brussels prosecutor appointed an investigating magistrate for murder. A condolence register was opened at Brussels City Hall from **14-16 October**, with a gathering at Cinquantenaire Park on **16 October at 11 am**. Meanwhile, the Urban Shield deployment shows **16.8% reduction in metro crime** and **7.1% reduction in railway stations** since April 2026, according to the federal Defence Minister. ## Hospitals: Four Brussels Communes Approve Merger

The Municipal Councils of Anderlecht, Ixelles, Saint-Gilles, and Etterbeek approved on **15 October 2026** the merger of five public Brussels hospitals under the "Brussels University Hospitals" label, effective **1 January 2027**. Votes were unanimous in Ixelles and Saint-Gilles; in Anderlecht, the PVDA and Team Fouad Ahidar abstained. The three institutions involved – Erasme, Bordet, Saint-Pierre, the Children's Hospital, and Iris Sud – will form an entity with approximately **1.5 billion euros** in consolidated turnover across **12 sites**. Only the VUB must still vote before the merger becomes effective.

## Housing: SLRB's **131 Million Euro** Allocation Confirmed in Committee

The State Secretary for Housing confirmed on **15 October in committee** that the **131 million euros** regional allocation to the SLRB would be paid, according to BX1 and BRUZZ. However, no official government decision was reported at that date. The dossier remains blocked by negotiations over social housing reforms and MR demands linked to the presidency of Foyer anderlechtois.

## Budget: Online Gambling Tax Maintained at **11%**

The Brussels Finance Minister reaffirmed on **12 October** in Parliament that the Region would not increase its online gambling and betting tax, as the government agreement does not provide for this. Flanders and Wallonia are raising this tax from **11% to 15%**. A Walloon operator moved its server to Brussels, confirming the real impact of the regional tax differential. ## Transport: Herrmann-Debroux Metro Station Renovated for **3 Million Euros**

Bruxelles Mobilité is renovating Herrmann-Debroux metro station in Auderghem, the terminus of line 5, as part of the Refresh programme. The work, budgeted at **3 million euros**, includes replacing four escalators, removing a kiosk, installing tactile guidance, and renewing fire detection systems. Completion is scheduled for **end of 2027**.

## Cleanliness: **70% of Collections Unshipped** on **12 October**

The **12 October** strike prevented many Bruxelles-Propreté collection rounds: approximately **70% of white bags** were not collected in Anderlecht and Saint-Gilles. The agency announced make-up rounds based on available resources. [See the Cleanliness domain →](/en/domains/cleanliness)

## Brownfields: Unfavourable Opinion on Josaphat; Region Decides

The concertation committee issued a **consultative unfavourable opinion on 15 October** on the building permit for the Josaphat brownfield in Schaerbeek. The municipality voted against; three members abstained. The project foresees up to **537 homes** on approximately **6.76 hectares**. The final decision rests with the Region, as the opinion is merely advisory. [See the Urban Planning domain →](/en/domains/urban-planning)

## Security: Recruitment of Regional Commissioner Ongoing

According to Belga on **15 October**, recruitment for the Regional Commissioner for Security and Drugs continues, with entry into office announced for autumn 2026. The Brussels Prosecutor General considers the position unnecessary given the national drug commissioner, while the government defends the need for regional coordination.

## Urban Planning: Fibre Box Regulation Submitted to State Council

Reform of the minor importance ordinance governing fibre optic boxes on façades has been submitted to the State Council. The draft aims to exclude cables from colour and volume rules, with no entry-into-force date communicated. This reform responds to practical deployment difficulties in Brussels, where most existing installations do not comply with current standards.

## Social: Humanitarian Hub Must Vacate Premises by **September 2027**

The Humanitarian Hub, which houses homeless migrants, must vacate its Port Avenue building by **September 2027**; no alternative location has been found. A permit application foresees constructing a training campus for Safe.brussels on **4,067 m²**, under public inquiry until **29 October**. The Hub lost **50% of its funding** since April 2026.
